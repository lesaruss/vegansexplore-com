The story in six steps

Why we're building this.

Ten years on the ground in Florida. Now the infrastructure to grow it.

Step 01 of 0601

This is for you.

You're a Vegan, a business owner building something plant-based, or someone who wants to fund the infrastructure that makes this movement grow.

This is for you.

We're building this to support businesses that are struggling, nonprofits that need backing, and individuals who can't find what they need.

Step 02 of 0602

The Vegan community is fractured.

People are doing incredible work all over the place, but no one is speaking to each other. We're not growing together; we're growing separately.

Step 03 of 0603

Why this exists.

Vegans Explore is the platform for the Vegan movement. We build the infrastructure for cities: a directory so you can find what's happening, events so you know where to show up, job boards so you can work in this movement, and volunteer opportunities so you can contribute where it matters.

Step 04 of 0604
500+organizations

Years on the ground.

Small potlucks. Expos. Multiple festivals. For 10 years in South Florida and Central Florida we canvassed, connected, showed up at events, hosted festivals and went into restaurants to spotlight them.

Now the work agents can do gets done by agents, and our members focus on the relationships: going into restaurants, making real connections.

You get a dashboard with opportunities to get involved, ways to level up, and a points system you can use to support other businesses. You can launch your own projects, with the full LESARUSS ecosystem behind you.

The points economy: Joining is free with a short survey, and the survey earns you 250 points. Points come from what you do: Bounties, competitions and taking part in the community and on the site. Use points to unlock a Guide (1,100 points), give a business a boost, or talk with your Guide. After that you buy only what you want: Guides ($11), merchandise and event tickets, and every purchase earns a 25-point thank-you. Points are never bought.
