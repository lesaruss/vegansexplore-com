Common Questions

What does the Passport include? −
The VE Passport is your key to the Vegans Explore network. It gives you access to all 8 active community hubs, member pricing on all partner opportunities, a digital membership badge for your profiles, and full community support. For just $11 one-time or $11/month, it opens the door to the entire Vegans Explore ecosystem.
What is the difference between Passport and Community Partner? +
The Passport is for individuals joining the community. Community Partner is for businesses and organizations that want monthly brand presence inside the VE network. Partners get a dedicated monthly marketing audit, a verified community listing, and two scheduled social collaborations per month with VE channels.
What is Marketing Services and who is it for? +
Marketing Services is for brands that want LESARUSS to handle their marketing entirely. We create web, video, and food photography content, manage your social media, plan growth strategy, and run custom campaigns under the VE brand umbrella. Pricing is custom based on scope. Contact us to discuss your needs.
Which cities are currently supported? +
Vegans Explore currently operates in South Florida, Washington DC, New York City, Los Angeles, Philadelphia, Orlando, Atlanta, and London. More cities are coming. Partners outside these markets are still welcome and will receive placement as new chapters launch.
How do I get started? +
Start with your Passport. Once you're in, the conversation about next steps is easy. Get your Passport for $11 (one-time or monthly), join the community, and then reach out if you'd like to explore partnership or marketing services opportunities.
