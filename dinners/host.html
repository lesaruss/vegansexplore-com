Why a dinner

Every city starts at one table.

For more than ten years I’ve been building South Florida’s Vegan community, first as SoFlo Vegans and now as Vegans Explore. Every city we go into starts the same way: a small table of the people others already follow, in a room that deserves to be known.

Not a festival, and not a pitch night. A dinner where they meet each other, hear what we’re building, and decide to build it with us. The restaurant that hosts that table is part of the story from the first night.

Sean A. RussellFounder, Vegans Explore. We’re a nonprofit, and we always will be.

The night

About two and a half hours.

  • ArriveGuests meet the table by introducing the person next to them.
  • DinnerYour chef’s two minutes, then your three-course tasting: appetizer, main and dessert.
  • After the main courseFive minutes from Sean on what we’re building, and a few guests’ answers read aloud.
  • Before they leaveEach guest picks one next step. We follow up with every one of them personally within 48 hours.
