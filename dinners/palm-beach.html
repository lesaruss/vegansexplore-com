Why you, why now

I wanted the people who move this city at one table.

For more than ten years I’ve been building South Florida’s Vegan community, first as SoFlo Vegans and now as Vegans Explore. In that time I’ve watched the same thing happen again and again: good people build incredible things, and almost nobody can find them. Everyone is building alone.

This dinner is where that changes. Before we open anything up to the wider community, I want the people who already make things happen here in one room: to hear what we’re building, to tell us what’s missing, and to decide if you want to build it with us.

You’re on this list because you’re one of those people.

Sean A. RussellFounder, Vegans Explore, with Ron Arrindell, South Florida Community Manager

What we’re building

One home base for Vegan South Florida.

The community has done incredible things with whatever tools were around. What’s been missing is a place built for us: to find each other, organize and grow.

The PulseLocal community stories, the things you’d usually hear about a week too late.
The DirectoryVegan and Vegan-friendly places, so people can find the businesses that get them.
The Community BoardAsk your city a question and find the people who can help.
Community NightsIn-person nights that move around the region, because a room is where it sticks.

South Florida is the first. Orlando, Philadelphia, New York and Los Angeles are next. This room is where it starts.
