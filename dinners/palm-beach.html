Why you, why now

I wanted the people who move this city at one table.

For more than ten years I’ve been building South Florida’s Vegan community, first as SoFlo Vegans and now as Vegans Explore. In that time I’ve watched the same thing happen again and again: good people build incredible things, and almost nobody can find them. Everyone is building alone.

Before we open anything up to the wider community, I want the people who already make things happen here in one room: to hear what we’re building, to tell us what’s missing, and to decide if you want to build it with us. You’re on this list because you’re one of those people.

Sean A. RussellFounder, Vegans Explore, with Ron Arrindell, South Florida Community Manager

    Questions

    Before you say yes.

    Is dinner really free?

    Yes, there’s no ticket. Dinner is hosted by our restaurant partner, and we take care of the staff. All we ask is that you come ready to talk about what we can build together.

    Do I have to pay anything?

    No. At the table I’ll offer Founding Membership, $11 one time, as one way to be part of this. It’s your call, and your seat never depends on it.

    Why do I need an account?

    Holding your seat creates your free Vegans Explore account. It’s how we confirm your seat, send you the venue and time, and keep you posted after the dinner. No payment, and you can leave any time.

    Can I contribute?

    Any time, from your dashboard, if you want to help us build faster. It’s a contribution to our community, not a tax-deductible donation.

    Can I bring someone?

    Seats are by name, so it’s just you at dinner. Bring your people on Saturday, October 24, as our guests, and tell us who should be at the next dinner.

    Why an answer in advance?

    So the night starts where you are. I’ll read a few answers aloud, and they shape what we build next.

    Can’t make it on the 23rd?

    Tell us on the seat slide. You can suggest someone to sit in your place, and you’ll be first on the list for the next dinner.
