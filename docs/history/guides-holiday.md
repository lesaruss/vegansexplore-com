## Give Back: Donate and Why support on every nonprofit, rescue and sanctuary (Sean, 2026-11-08, playbook `ve-holiday-guide`)

"Instead of sending people to their page, let's send them to the directory listing, and then on the directory listings, have the donation link
that's easy to see... at the very minimum, give a good 500 words of why somebody should support this nonprofit." The Holiday Guide's Give Back
cards open `/directory/<slug>` (places with a donation link first). A Nonprofits, Rescues or Sanctuaries listing shows **Donate** at the top of its
Overview (`giveCta` in `directory/listing.html`) and **Why support <name>** under About (`giveWhy`), both from `listings.details`: `support`
{text, words, sources, written_at, model, note}, `donation_url` (`donation_url_source` `ve-giveback` when we found it; one the team or the owner
set is never replaced) and `donation_link_code`, the organization's own tracked link (`ve_links` code `give-<slug>`, initiative `give-back`), so
`ve-links` counts and names every click. Written by edge function **`ve-giveback`** (verify_jwt off; super admin, admin token or cron secret;
`one` {slug, save?}, `batch` {slugs (25), wait?}): it reads their home, about and donate pages, collects giving links from their own site or known
giving platforms, and Claude (Opus 5.5, 16000, end_turn only) writes 400 to 550 words when the site has that much and less when it does not, from
their site only, never a tax claim, never calling them Vegan unless they say so; the donation link is picked only from the links found. A failed
try leaves `details.support_try` {at, why}. We never handle money. Pilot 2026-11-08: 20 Florida organizations, 19 written (218 to 466 words), 17
with a donation link (Piper's Pals and Nathans Family Farm show none on their sites), Little Bear Sanctuary's site did not open from the server.

the same day** ("I love it...
