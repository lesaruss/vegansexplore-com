## Step 0: The challenge

The first message to a listing has to come from @vegansexplore, so the relationship belongs to Vegans Explore, not to KJ. KJ must never hold
the password or be able to lock Sean out. Replies and follow-up can run anywhere the account's inbox is.

## B: What we know now

1. **Shared access is not offered on Sean's account.** Instagram's help (read 2026-11-18) ties it to the Meta One plans, which Meta says are
   "in limited testing" (Advanced: 4 people). Sean's app shows Meta Verified for Business Standard, Plus ($49.99) and Premium, with no Shared
   access on any of them. Paying for Plus would not unlock it.
2. **Meta Business Suite cannot start a new conversation.** It can answer anyone who wrote to the account, any time. A first message to
   someone new has to come from the Instagram app; after that, the thread shows in Business Suite and can be continued there
   ([respond.io](https://ja.respond.io/blog/instagram-business-chat), [SleekFlow](https://sleekflow.io/blog/instagram-messenger-api),
   2026-11-13 BOSS: Agorapulse and Sprout say the same).
3. **Every official tool (ManyChat and the like) can only reply**, including one private reply to someone who commented on our post. None
   can start a cold message ([Meta Instagram Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/instagram-messaging/overview)).
4. **Tools that take the password or share a logged-in session** (cookie-sharing extensions, cloud browsers) break Instagram's terms and risk
   the account being restricted ([mark360](https://mark360.ai/blog/instagram-dm-bot-guide-2026), [SetSmart](https://setsmart.io/blog/instagram-dm-automation)).
5. **What teams actually do** when a contractor must start conversations from a brand account: a device the brand owns, signed in by the
   owner, kept signed in. The contractor never sees the password; the owner holds two-factor, the email and the phone, and can sign the
   device out from Login activity at any time (2026-11-13 BOSS, revised order option 2).
