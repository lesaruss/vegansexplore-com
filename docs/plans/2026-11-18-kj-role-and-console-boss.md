Sean, 2026-11-18: "KJ is not really that much social media, more so than handling communications on this platform... reaching out to
the people on Instagram, getting the conversation going... checking in with people too... nurturing, maintenance... look for events, look
for people that we should be spotlighting... every day maybe look at a different chapter... follow people that we're going to be reaching
out to ahead of us reaching out to them." And: "it's okay to build the structure, but the content still needs to be finalized."
Builds on the Positions BOSS (2026-11-13) and the Directory Reach BOSS (2026-11-18). The console's structure is being built with every
word marked Draft; this BOSS settles the words and the rules. Nothing here is final until Sean reviews it.

## Step 0: The challenge

KJ needs one page that tells her, without a call: the big picture, exactly what is hers, what to do today, how far she can go on
Instagram and why, and where to record what happened, so Sean and Logan can see every conversation's status. Her work changes over time:
first reach (to December 1), then nurture and maintenance, plus scouting every chapter. The risks: an unclear role (she guesses, or
waits), an Instagram block (everything stops for one to two days), conversations we cannot see (Instagram DMs are not readable by our
system), and wording that treats a contractor like an employee.

## B: Best practice

1. **Instagram limits are unpublished; stay well under them.** Third-party guides (not Meta) put established accounts at roughly 150 to
   200 follows a day and 10 to 20 an hour, new accounts far lower; DMs to non-followers at 50 to 100 a day, sending faster than a person
   triggers blocks regardless of totals; a following list caps at 7,500; a block lasts hours to two days and repeats lengthen it; on a
   block, stop all outbound activity ([Chatplace](https://blog.chatplace.io/en/instagram-limits/), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-limits/),
   [Unfollr](https://www.unfollr.com/blog/instagram-follow-unfollow-limits), [Social Champ](https://www.socialchamp.com/blog/instagram-limits/),
   [Tagembed](https://tagembed.com/blog/instagram-restrictions-follow-like-comment/)). Our 2026-11-13 default (20 new DMs a day, 3 an
   hour) sits far below every estimate, on purpose: our account is the brand.
2. **Community work runs on a short daily routine and a weekly rhythm.** Inbox and mentions first, then outreach, about one to two hours a
   day; deeper relationship work weekly; a three-bullet recap each week ([Social Media Examiner trainer via NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/social-media-manager-daily-tasks/),
   [Socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/blog/daily-and-weekly-social-media-manager-checklist), [Sendible](https://www.sendible.com/resources/social-media-checklist-for-community-managers),
   [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-tasks/)).
3. **A DM pipeline needs named stages, each with a clear signal that moves someone forward; logging must be fast or it stops happening**
   ([Bitrix24](https://www.bitrix24.com/articles/from-reel-to-repeat-buyer-steps-to-create-a-high-converting-social-crm-funnel-in-24-hours.php),
   [Inflowave](https://inflowave.netlify.app/resources/instagram-dm-crm-vs-traditional-crm), [Make community](https://community.make.com/t/i-use-make-com-to-log-every-instantdm-conversation-without-creating-crm-clutter/114964)).
4. **Contractors get outcomes, deadlines, channels and reference material, not instructions on method** (misclassification risk);
   introduce them to who they work with; gather their feedback in the first weeks ([Remote](https://remote.com/blog/contractor-management/contractor-onboarding),
   [Oyster](https://www.oysterhr.com/library/contractor-onboarding-checklist), [TalentDesk](https://www.talentdesk.io/blog/contractor-onboarding-checklist)).
   Matches Sean's 2026-11-13 call: the queue is the scope of work; she picks her hours and her words.

## O: Outcome

- **Clarity:** KJ can say what is hers and what to do today after reading the page once; her questions in week one are about people,
  not about the job.
- **Reach (to December 1):** every listing in her lane followed ahead and messaged within the cap, or marked Can't reach with a reason.
- **Account health:** zero action blocks. One block pauses her new follows and DMs for 48 hours (replies keep going).
- **Visibility:** every conversation she starts has a status in the roster within the same day.
- **Results, per two-week pay period:** messages started, replies, visits and claims from her personal links, scout finds that went live.
  Targets set after her first week of real numbers.

## S: System

**Weekly rhythm (PDCA each week):** Monday the console fills her week; she works it; Friday a three-bullet recap (what worked, what got
stuck, one idea); Logan adjusts the openers and the queue for the next week; Sean sees the recap and the numbers.

### Her role (draft words for the Plan page)

**Social Media Manager (Instagram), Vegans Explore.** KJ is the voice that starts conversations. Every business, nonprofit and creator in
the Directory should hear, from a real person, that they are listed and the door is open before January 1. She opens those conversations
on Instagram, keeps them moving, and checks back in later. She also scouts a different chapter each weekday for events and people we
should be spotlighting. She works her own hours, in her own words; the queue is the work.

### Her responsibilities (outcomes, in order of priority)

1. **Answer first.** Replies and new messages to people she has reached, within one business day. Anything she cannot answer goes to Logan
   on the card (Ask Logan); decisions go to Sean.
1b. **Welcome every new follower and anyone who messages us** (added by Sean, 2026-11-18: "connecting with every single body that follows
   us... looking at what group they fit in, and then giving them the proper invitation"). Look at their profile, pick their group
   (Explorer, Restaurants and food, Other businesses, Nonprofits, Creators and media), add them in the console (quick add: handle and
   group), and send that group's pre-welcome page with their own link. Messages to us are answered first; new followers come before the
   outreach list and count toward the 20.
2. **Follow ahead.** Follow the people on the next two to three days of her list, so a follow-back can land before the message.
3. **Start conversations.** Message the people on today's list with the group's current opener (adapted in her words) and their own link.
4. **Record it.** One tap per person (status) and a note when there is something to know. This is her check-in and her pay record.
5. **Check in.** After the first push, the console brings back people who replied or claimed: a short check-in at about 7 days and again at
   about 30 days, and a thank-you when someone claims.
6. **Scout one chapter a day.** The console names the chapter; she looks at its local Vegan accounts, groups and pages and submits events
   and people or businesses we should spotlight. No quota; one strong find beats five weak ones.
7. **Note what she sees.** While looking at a profile: last post (this week, this month, 1 to 6 months, 6+ months, no posts, account not
   found, looks closed). Closed or quiet places go to Directory review.
8. **Friday recap.** Three bullets.

### The limits (draft words for the cap box)

**20 new conversations a day, no more than 3 an hour. Up to 40 follows a day, no more than 10 an hour.** Instagram does not publish its
limits and blocks accounts that act faster than a person or reach out to many strangers at once. A block stops every new follow and
message on the account for one to two days, and repeat blocks last longer. These numbers keep the Vegans Explore account safe. Replies to
people who wrote back have no cap. If Instagram ever shows a warning or a block: stop new follows and messages, tell Logan, and keep
answering replies. No quota from us beyond this.

### The stages (one tap each, a clear signal for each)

| Stage | Signal that puts someone here |
|---|---|
| To follow | On her follow-ahead list |
| Followed | She tapped Followed |
| Follows us | She saw they followed back (Instagram does not tell our system) |
| Messaged | She tapped Message sent |
| Replied | They wrote back (her tap) or replied to an email (automatic) |
| Interested | They asked a question or opened their link (link visit is automatic) |
| Claimed | They claimed their page (automatic) |
| Check-in due | 7 or 30 days after Replied or Claimed (automatic) |
| Not now | They said no or not yet (her tap, with a note); back in 60 days |
| Can't reach | No Instagram account, closed, or no way to message (her tap, with the reason) |

Unsubscribed and bounced come from email automatically and are shown, never messaged on Instagram about email.

### The roster (what each row shows)

Thumbnail (logo, rounded square), name, group, chapter, channels available (email, Instagram, website), stage, last contact (date and
channel), times contacted, opened or clicked (email), replied, claimed, her last note. Filters: group, stage, channel, chapter; search;
sort by last contact or stage. Each row opens the person's full history and links to their Directory listing.

### Her week (how the list is made)

Monday to Friday, filled automatically from the Directory Reach order: people who engaged with a tag post, Sean's pins, Instagram-only
listings by group (restaurants first, the largest), then no reply to email after 5 days. 20 to message per weekday, the next 2 to 3 days
to follow ahead. Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday are off. Sean can pin anyone to the top.

## S: Simplify

- She never decides who is next: the list does. She decides the words.
- One tap records the stage; a note only when it matters.
- One opener per group, versioned with the email; when the offer changes, her opener changes the same day.
- The page is the job description: if it is not on the page, it is not expected.

## Continuous improvement

Week one is the test: her recap plus the numbers (follow-backs, replies per opener, any warnings). Adjust the caps only after two clean
weeks (Positions BOSS rule), the openers weekly, the scout rotation if a chapter has nothing.

## Decisions for Sean (each with a default)

1. **Follow cap: 40 a day, 10 an hour** (DMs stay at 20 and 3). Default: yes.
2. **Check-ins at about 7 and 30 days** after a reply or claim. Default: yes.
3. **Scout: one chapter per weekday, no quota.** Default: yes.
4. **Friday three-bullet recap** on the page. Default: yes.
5. **Time guide: "about an hour on weekdays, your hours"** shown on the page (her pay is per period, not per hour). Default: yes.
6. **Welcoming new followers counts toward the 20 a day** (they follow us, so it lands in their main inbox; replies to people who wrote
   to us never count). Default: yes, new followers first, then the outreach list.
7. **The console gets a quick add and an Inbound list** (handle, group, matched to a listing when there is one). Default: yes, built
   after the panel.

## Conclusion

KJ's page is her job description, her week and her record in one place: a short role, eight responsibilities ranked by priority, the
caps with the reason, ten stages with clear signals, and a roster everyone can read. Confidence: high on the structure and the stages;
medium on the follow cap (no official number exists, so we start low and raise only after two clean weeks).
