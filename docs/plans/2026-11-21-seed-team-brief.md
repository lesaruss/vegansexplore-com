# The World Vegan Expo: a conversation with the Seed team

*For Sean to bring to the Seed Food and Wine team, before the committees go public. One page.*

## Why we're reaching out first

Seed shaped how I think about Vegan events. I spent seven years as your marketing chair, and the way you built the
committees is the model I want to follow. Before we announce anything about our committees, I wanted you to hear it
from me and tell me what you think.

## What we're building

- **The World Vegan Expo**, planned for **January 20 to 24, 2028**, every two years. One host city, not yet picked.
- **Five days:** a trade day, three public days and an impact day, with a chef dinner every night and the Vegans Explore Awards.
- **Villages:** a Food Village, an Exhibitor Hall, a World Food Pavilion, an Education Village, a Causes Village, a Market,
  Wellness and Fitness, Creators and Media, and a Community Hub.
- **Vegans Explore** is our platform for finding everything Vegan, city by city, run by LESARUSS Inc.
  The Guide is live at vegansexplore.com/expo.

## What we're taking from Seed

- **Volunteer committees, each led by a Chair:** Marketing, Vendors, Exhibitors and Sponsors, Programming, Sustainability,
  Community, Causes and Volunteers, Creators and Media, Host City and Travel.
- **Invitation first:** influencers, media owners and community leaders.
- **Good standing:** one meeting a month (on Zoom) and contributing.
- **An All Access Pass** for members in good standing, from the day they join to the Expo.
- **Paid work posted inside the committees first,** and people rise on their own ambition.

## What we'd love from you

1. **Your honest take:** what worked at Seed, what you'd do differently, and what we're missing.
2. **Your ideas on speakers:** how you brought them in without paying fees.
3. **Whether you'd like a part in it:** on a committee, as a partner, or in whatever way fits.

Nothing about the committees is public yet. We're holding them for members until we've talked with you.

*Sean A. Russell, founder, Vegans Explore*
