# Panel: outreach from @vegansexplore without handing over the account (2026-11-18)

Sean: "let's run a panel of social media experts... give us some solutions on how to work this out." After he rejected KJ's own account,
password sharing and a shipped phone (BOSS `2026-11-18-kj-account-access-boss.md`). Canon `canon-panel`; four independent lenses on one
brief (scratchpad panel-brief): agency head, Instagram DM growth strategist, Meta platform specialist, community and creator partnerships lead.
Internal thinking lenses, not real people. Advice, not canon; Sean decides.

## Unanimous (4 of 4)

**Flip who opens the conversation: feature them first, they write to us.** Meta lets a delegate reply to anyone who has contacted the
account, but never lets a delegate start a cold DM. So @vegansexplore makes the first move in public, and KJ works the replies.
1. Sean adds KJ in Business Suite (Settings, People) with partial access to the Instagram account: Content and Messages (and Comments). No
   password, no full control; Sean removes her in one tap and sees every message.
2. Each day KJ turns her cards into a "Now on Vegans Explore" carousel and stories from @vegansexplore that tag or mention the places really
   featured in them. A mention or tag notifies them from the brand.
3. When they reply, thank us, comment or reshare, the thread is in the brand's inbox; KJ answers in Business Suite with their tracked link and
   logs it. Their thread lands in the main inbox, not Requests.
Why it works: every touch is @vegansexplore in public, KJ is invisible, nobody holds the keys, and a feature is a gift where a cold DM is a
request. "The relationship belongs to where the history lives and who shows up publicly" (partnerships lens).

## Strong support (3 of 4)

- **Comment-to-DM on every feature post:** "Featured? Comment PAGE and we'll send your listing." An official tool (ManyChat, Sean connects it
  with his own login) sends one private reply with the tracked link; KJ continues the thread.
- **Sean's short send block as the backstop:** places that never answer a feature after about 7 days go to a send queue KJ prepares (opener
  written, link ready); Sean follows and sends from his own phone, about 10 to 15 minutes a day. The only way inside Meta's rules to start a
  cold DM without Shared access.
- **Ask Meta Verified support, in writing,** whether @vegansexplore can join the Meta One test or get Shared access, and whether there is a
  waitlist (Meta platform lens; about 15 minutes; run alongside, not instead).

## Splits and calls

- **KJ's role:** the partnerships lens would rename it Community Producer (features, collab reels, city roundups the brand posts; a clause
  that every contact and thread belongs to Vegans Explore). Call: keep the title, change the work to exactly that; it is what the $250 buys.
- **Tag volume:** earlier panel said one city post a day, 10 tags or fewer, and tags count only as Notified. Call: real features only (each
  tagged place is in the post), one carousel plus stories a day, up to 20 tags, spaced out; a reply counts as Engaged, a tag alone stays
  Notified.

## To check in the app (before Monday)

Whether Business Suite's story composer offers the mention sticker on this account (if not, Sean posts the stories from KJ's drafts); the
tag and mention limits per post and story; whether Business Suite lets a delegate send collab invites.

## Recommendation (default if Sean does not override)

Feature first as the engine, comment-to-DM on every post, Sean's 10-minute send block only for places that stay quiet after a week, and a
written ask to Meta for Shared access. KJ gets Business Suite partial access (Content, Messages, Comments) only.
