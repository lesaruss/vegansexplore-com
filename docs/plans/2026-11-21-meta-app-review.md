# Meta App Review: Vegans Explore app (comment-to-DM), 2026-11-21

Proven live with a tester account the same day (comment B12, DM with the tracked link, public reply, click landed on the B12 page).
App Review lets it work for everyone who comments, not only people with a role on the app.

## Request only these three (the other six were removed from the request)

**instagram_basic**
Vegans Explore uses instagram_basic to read the basic profile of its own Instagram account (@vegansexplore) and the username of people who
comment on its posts, so a reply goes to the right person and our own comments are skipped.

**instagram_manage_comments**
Vegans Explore posts short videos with a free guide offer ("Comment B12 and we'll DM you the free Guide"). The app reads new comments on
@vegansexplore posts through the comments webhook, and when a comment contains the keyword it posts one short public reply under it
("Sent! Check your DMs"). It does not read or reply to comments anywhere else.

**instagram_manage_messages**
When someone comments the keyword, the app sends that person one private reply (the comment's private reply) with a link to the free guide
on vegansexplore.com. One message per person per keyword. It never messages people who did not comment, and a person who writes back is
answered by a person on the Vegans Explore team.
