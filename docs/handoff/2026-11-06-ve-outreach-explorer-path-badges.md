# ve-outreach: Explorer path and badges (idea to lock, Nov 6, 2026, Eastern)

Playbook: `ve-outreach` (`ve-pathways` was merged into it). Record 5f3ce432 in `playbook_records`. Raised by Sean in the `ve-podcast-studio` chat. **Not locked yet.**
Proposed as v7 of `canon-ve-door-guides`.

## Sean's idea, in his words
"What if we created an explorer path, which I kind of feel should be everybody's path... the comprehensive survey as the door through...
This whole thing should take 5 minutes... after they walk through the door... they see all of the pathways... select the paths that make
sense to you... once you get them completed, you'll earn a badge... like Girl Scout and Boy Scout badges... It's almost like college. It's
the core classes, and after you get to your sophomore year or your junior year, you get to take the actual classes you came for."
Example: Elle Jimenez is a content creator, hosts a community (group) and runs events. Sean doesn't fit one bucket either.

## What it means
1. **Explorer is everyone's path.** One short guided intro to what Vegans Explore is (about 5 minutes, exciting, not overwhelming), ending in the survey as the door.
2. **Every path visible after the door.** Content Creator, Group Leader, Event Host, Business / Restaurant, Nonprofit, Ambassador and so on.
   Preview each one and thumb through its Guide. Finish it at your own pace. A finished intake earns that path's **badge** and makes you eligible for its opportunities. Badges are collected, not chosen once.
3. **Core classes.** Every Guide's intake starts with the Explorer questions. Anyone who has done Explorer skips to the path's own questions.
4. **After the door.** Updates, reminders and special opportunities based on the badges held and the paths previewed.
