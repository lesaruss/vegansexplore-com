## What the standard says, in short

- Certifies named dishes and the kitchen practice behind them, never "this restaurant is Vegan."
- Gates G1 to G8 must all pass: ingredient trace, hidden in process, cross-contact, labeling honesty, staff answers, change control, access and honesty, records.
- Level 1: all gates, at least 3 dishes, no scoring in year one. Level 2: 60 points and dedicated utensils and prep. Level 3: 80 points and dedicated cooking equipment for every certified dish.
- Stamp wording: "Plant-Based Dish, Pre-Vegan Certified," with "N of M menu items certified" and a line that the restaurant also serves animal products.
- The fee buys the audit and one re-test, never the outcome. Verifier paid per visit, not per pass. Working price about $299 and $199, not final.
- Taste is not scored. Tasting events give feedback anonymous to the restaurant and known to us.
- Distributors and chambers pay and receive nothing. Pilot first.
