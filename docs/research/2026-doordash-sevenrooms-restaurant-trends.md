## Source
- **Publisher:** DoorDash with SevenRooms (owned by DoorDash); made with Datalily.
- **Year:** 2026 (surveys March 2026). Next edition expected: 2027. Check again: 2027-05-01.
- **Method:** two Dynata surveys for DoorDash, March 2026: 3,001 US consumers, 509 US restaurant operators. Respondents not paid by DoorDash.
  Plus DoorDash platform data (Jan to Dec 2025) and SevenRooms platform data (Oct 2025 to Mar 2026). Survey data unless marked.
- **Flag: vendor research.** The report sells DoorDash Marketplace, Commerce Platform, Reservations and SevenRooms. Quote as "DoorDash found",
  never as our result. Platform-data lifts (6%, 13%, 16%, 27%, 16X) are DoorDash's or SevenRooms' own customers only.
- **Sectors:** restaurants. **Brands:** Vegans Explore (BrandDesk, restaurant pitch), LESARUSS AI (AI discovery), investor decks.

## Corrections logged 2026-11-09
Marketing pages and early web summaries misstated the report. Do not use these: "only 39% of operators have updated their listings" (the 39% is
operators who updated menu information for AI visibility, row 12); "79% of delivery customers also dine in" (79% is repeat delivery orders, row 6;
the cross-channel numbers are rows 34 and 35); "30% of owners cite delivery as the top acquisition driver" (no percentage in the report, row 8).
