# Door Guides: free with an email, your voice with the Passport (Sean's idea, 2026-11-02, not locked)

A concept session with V. Not locked, not built. The Vegan Events Guide is the first; the Creator, Nonprofit and Vegan Restaurant
Guides follow once the events work is done.

## Sean, in his words (condensed)

- "Turn these... flywheels into our universe, into free guides... all they got to do is give us their email address to unlock it...
  We don't scare them away by saying it's $11 and they never click it."
- Not every Guide: "the dairy guide is a dairy guide." These are the ones that walk people through the door.
- What the $11 unlocks is their voice: record their answers, a question of the month, their story, the good news or what they are
  struggling with, "something you want to tell the community". We splice the clips into podcast segments.
- "That way the guide's living and they're coming back to it... frequently, to see what opportunities there are."
- Nobody has paid for these Guides yet, so we can change them cleanly.

## V's shape for it (Sean's to adjust)

1. **Open, no email:** the Overview and the one free example (public, so Google finds it).
2. **Free with an email:** the whole Guide to read. The email makes them a Taster (the account we already built), with a welcome
   series tailored to that Guide.
3. **The $11 Passport: your voice and your tools.**
   - Record in the browser: the Guide's questionnaire (the Events Guide's six host questions), the **question of the month**, your
     story, good news, or what you are struggling with.
   - Every recording is read by the team before it airs, with the release we already use (our shows and site, name credited,
     taken down on request), and points once approved.
   - Approved clips become podcast segments (Host Notes for events; one per Guide), credited on the person's People profile.
   - The doing tools: saving, checklists that remember, points, and, for events, ticketing and messaging guests.
4. **Always free, never behind the Passport:** the event claim and the sponsorship application (they bring business).

**Living:** each door Guide gets a **This month** section: the question of the month, open opportunities (sponsorships, gigs,
bounties) and the latest segment made from members' clips. That is the reason to come back.

## The Events Guide first

It already has the six host questions recorded in the browser, the release and 100 points (built 2026-11-01). The change is mostly who
can open what: reading moves to free with an email, recording moves to the Passport, and a question of the month and This month are
added. Then the same pattern goes to the Creator, Nonprofit and Vegan Restaurant Guides.

## The one rule change it needs

"Every Guide is $11 or 1,100 points" becomes "every Guide is $11 or 1,100 points, except the door Guides: free with an email, your
voice and tools with the Passport."
