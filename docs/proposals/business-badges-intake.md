# Business badges: the intake questions (draft for Sean)

Playbook: `ve-business-badges` (canon `canon-ve-business-badges`, locked 2026-11-06). Draft only. Nothing built until Sean approves.

These questions go in every business intake (Restaurant, Events, Pre-Vegan Business, Creator, Nonprofit). The owner's own answers set the badge. We never judge it for them.

## Question 1. Is everything you sell plant-based?
- Yes, everything
- No, we also sell some animal products
- Not yet, we are working toward it

## Question 2. (Only if "Yes, everything") Which word fits your business best?
- **Vegan.** We run it for the animals. We keep animal products out of everything we do, not only the food.
- **Plant-based.** All our food is plants. That is our focus.

Note under the question: "Both are respected. There is no wrong answer. Pick the one that is true for you."

## Question 3. (Only if "Vegan") Do you keep animal products out beyond the plate?
For example: honey, sugar made with bone char, wine or beer filtered with animal products, leather, cleaning products.
- Yes, all of it
- Mostly, we are still checking a few things
- Not yet

## Question 4. (Only if "No" or "Not yet" on Question 1) Would you like help with your plant-based items?
- Yes, I want to hear about Pre-Vegan Certified for some items
- Yes, I want help adding plant-based items
- Not right now

## What the owner sees after
- "Your badge will be **100% Vegan**. Our team checks it before it shows. You can change your answers any time."
- "Your badge will be **100% Plant-based**. It shows on your page now. You can change your answers any time."
- "Your badge will be **Pre-Vegan**: you offer plant-based options. Want some items certified? Tell us."

## How answers set the badge
| Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Badge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yes | Vegan | Yes | 100% Vegan (after our team checks) |
| Yes | Vegan | Mostly or Not yet | 100% Plant-based for now, and our team follows up to help them get to 100% Vegan |
| Yes | Plant-based | (not asked) | 100% Plant-based |
| No or Not yet | (not asked) | (not asked) | Pre-Vegan |

Pre-Vegan Certified is never set by these answers. It comes only from our item checks (Standard V1.2).

## Logan's notes
- Today every intake asks "How Vegan is what you run?" with Everything Vegan / Mostly Vegan / Plant-based options / Planning. Question 1 replaces it. "Mostly Vegan" goes away, since it is not a badge.
- Answers carry over between intakes (the shared answers system), so an owner answers once.
- Changing an answer from Vegan back to Plant-based changes the badge at once, no penalty, no note to anyone.
