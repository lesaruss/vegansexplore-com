## Signal and the 100 promise
- Signal is Web plus Google Search Console, page speed, tag manager and the technical fixes. Read-only first; **Search Console is not connected today**, so connecting it is the first Signal task.
- The 100 promise is **only on the managed subscription** (the $500 a month tier, site on our platform, our template). DIY is a HubSpot-style tool with scores and no promise. Our own brands go to 100 first; where we cannot, we do not promise.
- Everything we control is at 100, every fixable issue is fixed on a schedule (daily, weekly or monthly by plan), a short report says what was done, and anything blocking 100 is listed with a plain reason.
- Auto-fix covers safe classes only (redirects, titles and descriptions, sitemap, image size, alt text) on sites we host, each logged with an undo.
