## Notes

- Every section named is one on the new home (the mock, `mocks/depot/`). Nothing promised that doesn't exist once step 1 is built.
- **Where everything went (Sean's notes, 2026-11-07)**: Ambassadors, Founding Members, Nonprofit and Group Partners, Invites, Referral claims, Profiles, Community Managers, Links, Outreach, Business outreach, People, Members, Leads and Role applications go to BrandDesk > People; until BrandDesk can hold each one, they sit under a People tile marked Moving to BrandDesk, with no tour chapter. Submissions and Watchlist go to Bounties. Secret Shopper goes to Directory. Preview journeys, Onboarding and the Dashboard checklist go to Guides. Community Insights goes to BrandDesk Insights, Platform health to BrandDesk's Signal, Feedback to the Blueprint's small-fixes queue. Guest reviews (the old Guest Passport queue) goes to the trash bin once checked empty.
- **Ideas stays in the Depot as its own section** (Sean, 2026-11-07: "this is where the ideas should live instead of the blueprint, because I feel like it's kind of buried in the blueprint"), with Feedback beside it.
- BrandDesk is its own tool and has its own tour, so it is not in this one.
- The Depot is for the team, so there is no members-only part; anyone who can open the Depot sees the whole tour.
