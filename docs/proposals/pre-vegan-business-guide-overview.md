## On the page (visitors and members see the same story; members get the checklist ticked)

**Eyebrow:** Vegans Explore Guides for business
**Title:** Adding plant-based options? You're in the right place.

**1. The elephant in the room**
You might be worried you'll get it wrong. You'll call something plant-based and someone will find butter in it. Or
you'll put a dish on the menu that nobody orders. Or you'll feel like a Vegan crowd is waiting to judge you. That's
normal, and it's exactly why this Guide exists.

**2. You're in the right place**
This Guide is for businesses that aren't Vegan and want to offer plant-based options: coffee shops, gyms, juice bars,
cafes, caterers and restaurants. You don't have to change who you are to start.

**3. Why this Guide exists**
We started Pre-Vegans as a pathway: a place where people who aren't Vegan yet are met with education and compassion,
not judgment. This Guide is that same pathway for businesses. Plant-based options are where it starts. Where it goes
is up to you.

**4. What to expect**
- What to add first for your kind of business, what to stock and what to watch for
- Who sells it ready-made, every supplier with an honest label
- A recipe for each item if you'd rather make it
- The right words for your menu, so customers trust what they read
- How people find you on Vegans Explore, and how to claim your page

**5. When you finish**
You'll have your first plant-based items picked, someone to buy them from or a recipe to make them, the right words on
your menu, and your page on Vegans Explore where people are looking. Finishing means five things, and you can tick them
as you go:
1. Pick your kind of business
2. Choose your first items
3. Line up a supplier or a recipe for each
4. Set your menu wording
5. Claim your page

**6. How to use this Guide**
- The tabs across the top are the tour. Go in order with Back and Next, or jump to what you need.
- Press Play under my name and I'll talk you through each page.
- Save anything to My list so it's there next time.
- If a page is locked, take the short survey and the whole Guide opens.

**7. It's easier than it looks**
It's free. The survey takes a few minutes. There is no wrong place to start, and nobody is grading you.

**8. Ask me**
Stuck on something? Ask me. If I can't answer it, I'll pass it to our team and a real person will get back to you.
