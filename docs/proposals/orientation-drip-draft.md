# ve-outreach-buckets: Orientation, the always-on drip after joining (DRAFT for Sean to read)

Playbook `ve-outreach-buckets` (canon `canon-ve-outreach-roster-campaigns`: "After joining, people move into Orientation: now that you are in, what
do you do; a slow drip of what is open to them, each note offering the Daily Pulse"). **Nothing is built or sent.** Words only.

Logan's defaults, Sean's to change:
- **One Orientation for everyone**, with one line that changes by the door they came through (restaurant, business, nonprofit, group, brand,
  or a regular member). One set of words is easier to keep true than five.
- **Four notes**, a week apart, starting the week after their door's own notes end (the restaurant door's Day 7 note, the Events Guide's Day 7).
- From the Vegans Explore team, weekdays from 10 AM Eastern, through `email-send` brand vegans-explore. Stops when they unsubscribe.
- Every note ends with the same offer: get your city's Daily Pulse. Only for someone not already subscribed.
- No prices, no points to spend, no counts. Anything Coming is said as Coming.
