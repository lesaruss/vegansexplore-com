# Panel: Vegans Explore Travel and Global Tour 2.0 (2026-11-11)

Sean: "Each of those stops could also be a tour... group tours... local tour guides who will be facilitating everything... the tours
essentially are part of what funds me being out there, making business deals"; then "tours start June 2027 for the summer Europe leg,
seat the panel". Inputs: `2026-11-11-travel-partnerships-boss.md` and the Global Tour master plan (Google Doc, updated 2025-03-26; pasted by
Sean). Five panelists, each briefed separately so none saw the others: a small-group tour operator, a travel-law and compliance advisor, a
tourism bureau marketer, a Vegan boutique hotel owner, a media and brand strategist. Their words are summarized; they are model panelists,
not real people, and the legal points are for counsel to confirm.

## Where all five agree

1. **The travel directory is sound**: listed on merit, direct-first booking, labelled paid links, destination data.
2. **A licensed tour operator is the seller of record and holds every dollar of traveler money.** Never LESARUSS, never Vegans Explore.
   Deposits go to the operator's trust or escrow account; the operator carries EU/UK package-travel insolvency protection. Counsel to
   confirm whether Vegans Explore's own marketing needs Florida Seller of Travel registration or fits an exemption (Fla. Stat. 559.927,
   559.935), and check California, Washington, Hawaii and Iowa.
3. **Drop "tours fund the founder's presence."** Four of five named it the biggest risk: deposits spent before a tour runs, guests who feel
   misled, bureaus who expect the host on camera. A dedicated Vegans Explore host on every departure; Sean's LESARUSS travel on its own
   budget (a CPA's view); Vegans Explore's per-guest share paid only after departure; the terms say plainly that Sean joins some dinners
   and does not lead the tour.
4. **Shrink 2027 to a pilot.** One co-branded Europe departure, one region, one or two Vegan hotel bases, 7 to 9 nights, 12 to 18 guests
   (minimum 10), not ten cities on two continents. Asia moves to 2028, only if Europe sells.
5. **Keep the wall between money and selection, in writing**: a published rubric and reviewers who never see sponsor, bureau or hotel
   contracts; paid money buys labelled visibility, never a listing or the mark; "Travel supported by X; X had no say in what we chose."

## The numbers they gave

- **Tour price** about $4,500 to $6,000 a guest sharing, single supplement $1,000 to $1,600 (World Vegan Travel's 2023 Garden Route ran
  $4,995 to $5,495 shared). $750 deposit, refunded in full if the minimum is not met; monthly payments; balance 90 days out; go or no-go
  120 days out.
- **Vegans Explore's share** 10 to 15% or a flat $400 to $600 a guest, nothing paid upfront, one free host seat per 12 guests, film rights,
  named hotel partners; exclusivity only on co-branded departures.
- **Hotel referral terms** 8 to 10% of room revenue on completed stays, paid monthly after checkout, tracked by a code in the hotel's own
  engine; the member perk is a welcome drink, breakfast or late checkout, not a rate cut; no fees, no exclusivity.
- **Hotel group blocks** contracted by the operator: net rates with half board, 25% deposit at signing, 50% at 90 days, release at 60
  days, up to 20% attrition, 2-night minimum; July and August at rack rate with a 3-night minimum. **Contracts signed by January 2027 or
  the rooms are gone.**
- **Tourism bureaus**: no co-funding of 2027 international stops. Start at home (Visit Lauderdale, Greater Miami CVB), in-kind first (a
  hosted media trip), any cash a modest content fee for set deliverables; their fiscal years start October 1, so pitch FY2028 between
  February and May 2027 with 90 days of tracked data (clicks by destination, share of visitors from outside the market, email size and
  opens, podcast downloads) and one business case study. European boards for 2028, with that report.
- **The film**: one city shot deeply as a 22 to 26 minute pilot plus a sizzle reel, built on two or three local Vegan founders; releases,
  music and brand placements in writing; YouTube first, the documentary pitched once the pilot shows an audience. Sponsor and bureau
  support disclosed on screen and spoken at the start (FTC, 16 CFR 255).

## Where they differ: the month

Sean set June 2027. The operator says September or October (seven months is short for a first-time seller; a new seller fills 30 to 50%
of a departure). The hotel owner says late May, early June or September (July and August at rack rate), and only with contracts by
January 2027. June can work only if the operator is signed and rooms contracted by January.

## What the hotel owner wants first

A personal email from someone who has stayed or eaten there, showing the listing already live; no mass mailing; the directory deal before
any tour ask; the operator named before any rooms are held.
