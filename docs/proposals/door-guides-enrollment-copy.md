## 3. Our story (a block inside Overview)

**We are not new to this movement.**

I spent more than ten years on the ground in the Vegan movement. I ate at more Vegan places than I can count and met the people behind them.

That is why Vegans Explore looks the way it does. You can hear it in our shows, read it in our interviews, and see it in a Directory built from places and people we have actually been with.

I keep my pulse on everything happening in the movement, from activism to the food industry to tech and business, and I share what works. That is how I treat the movement, the same way I look after a community I care about.

It is what we have done with SoFlo Vegans since 2016. That community is still ours, and Vegans Explore is the same work with better tools.

Your page on Vegans Explore is built to be better than a website you have to maintain alone, because it sits next to everything we have made. We are building it with the people who join early. If you are one of them, you help decide what comes next.

What we will not say: that we are big. We are not claiming reach we cannot show. We are asking for your help to get there, so we can support the community properly.

Below it on the page: our four shows (played in place), a few owner interviews, and a link to the Daily Pulse.

### Restaurant survey (17 questions in four parts, about 10 minutes; 5 required, the rest optional, points on each)

First screen: **Your answers make your page, and the whole Directory, better for the people who use it.** Each story answer has the choice **Can be shown on my page** or **Just for Vegans Explore** (the member profile rule). A person reads every survey before the door opens.

Part 1, Your story
1. Are you the founder, an owner, or part of the team? (required)
2. Why did you open it? Why are you running a Vegan business? (required, long)
3. How did it start? (optional, long)
4. What are you proudest of? Brag a little. (optional, long)
5. What is the one dish, or thing, you want people to try first? (optional)
6. A fun fact about your place that people would not guess. (optional)

Part 2, Your place
7. How Vegan is your menu? (required: 100% Vegan, Vegan options, Vegan friendly)
8. How long have you been open? (optional)
9. Who comes in? (optional, pick any: locals, regulars, tourists, families, Vegans, Pre-Vegans)

Part 3, Being found and supported
10. Where do most new customers find you today? (optional, pick any)
11. Are you happy with your website? (optional: yes, it is OK, no, I do not have one)
12. What do you wish you had more support with as a business owner in the Vegan space? (required, long)
13. What would help most right now? (optional, pick any: more customers, a better page, photos, events, social media, a website, ordering, someone to do it for me)

Part 4, Tools and your community
14. Which point of sale or ordering system do you use, and what do you think of it? (optional: Square, Toast, Clover, other, none, then a line)
15. Do you host events or nights, and would you like to see more events in your community? (required)
16. Do you recommend any other businesses or nonprofit organizations we should work with in your area? (optional; two slots and a plus button, up to five: search the Directory or type a name and website; anything we do not have becomes a lead the team checks)
17. One thing you wish people knew about your place. (optional)

What happens to the answers: story answers marked Can be shown feed their page (a Their story section, to be built; today a page has the one line and the description). Question 14 tells us which point of sale platforms to build relationships with. Question 16 feeds New places. Questions 12, 13 and 15 feed what we build and which events we promote.
