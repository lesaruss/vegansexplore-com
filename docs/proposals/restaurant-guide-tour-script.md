# The Vegan Restaurant Guide tour: Liz's script (draft for Sean)

Canon `canon-ve-guide-tour-standard` (locked 2026-11-07). Pilot Guide. **Nothing is recorded until Sean reads and approves this.**
Each chapter is recorded on its own (Liz's roster-profile voice, Vegan spelled "Veegan" for the recording, checked word for word with stt).
Played back to back, the chapters are the tour on the Overview. Played alone, a section's chapter is that page's **Summarize this page**.
About 290 words, roughly 1 minute 50 seconds at Liz's pace (check against the recordings; if it runs over two minutes, chapters 3 to 10 get shorter).
