## 1. What is certified

1.1 Only the dishes listed in Schedule A, as made at the location named, are certified ("Certified Dishes"). The certification never
means the Restaurant, its kitchen or its menu is Vegan.

1.2 Certification means each Certified Dish passed all eight gates of the Pre-Vegan Certification Standard (the "Standard", Schedule F,
version in force on the signing date): G1 ingredient evidence, G2 nothing hidden in process, G3 a protected cooking path, G4 accurate use on
every channel, G5 staff knowledge, G6 change approval, G7 access and corrective action, G8 records.

1.3 It is not a health, nutrition, allergen, food-safety, laboratory or whole-kitchen claim. The Restaurant stays solely responsible for its
food, its allergen practices and its compliance with food-safety law.

## 2. Fees

2.1 Certification is **$250 for each dish**, paid before the desk review starts. It covers the desk review, the Verifier's visit, one community
tasting night, the 12-month term and one courtesy check during the term. There are no free certifications.

2.2 Renewal is **$150 for each dish** for each further 12-month term.

2.3 A fee pays for the review, never the outcome. It is not refunded because a dish does not pass. A dish that does not pass may be submitted
again at the same price.

2.4 Promotion, recipe help, sourcing, staffing help and the print service (Schedule G) are separate, optional orders and never affect
certification.

## 3. The process

3.1 The Restaurant completes the private pre-assessment, signs this agreement, and sends the evidence package (Schedule C) at least 10
business days before the visit.

3.2 A desk reviewer checks the evidence. A Verifier, who is not the desk reviewer, visits, watches each Certified Dish being made, checks
storage, shelves and unlabeled containers, questions at least two staff, and takes photos (no audio recording). A third person, the approver,
decides in writing. The approver cannot overrule a failed gate.

3.3 Every critical staff question must be answered correctly. One wrong critical answer fails G5 for the visit.

## 4. Term, checks and renewal

4.1 The term is 12 months from the approval date. There is no grace period: when the term ends, the mark ends.

4.2 **The term never pauses.** If the Restaurant takes a Certified Dish off its menu for any reason, the term keeps running. If the same,
unchanged dish returns before the term ends, its certification still counts until the original end date. Time off the menu is never added
back.

4.3 The fee includes one courtesy check during the term to confirm the dish is still made as documented. We may also send secret shoppers at
any time, who order the dish and ask staff about it, and make one unannounced midterm spot check, and targeted checks after a complaint, a change of owner, chef or key
equipment, or a recall that may touch a Certified Dish. The Restaurant gives our Verifier access during opening hours.

4.4 Renewal is a new audit under the version of the Standard then in force.

## 5. Changes (G6)

5.1 The Restaurant tells us in writing **before** any change to a Certified Dish: an ingredient, a brand, a supplier, a recipe, a process,
the equipment or the cooking path.

5.2 **Any change voids that dish's certification.** The changed dish needs a new review before it carries the mark again **[at the renewal
price]**. Not knowing the rule is no excuse: it is in this agreement and in the staff training.

5.3 **Hiding a change ends everything.** If a Certified Dish is served changed without telling us first, or the Restaurant otherwise misleads
diners or us about a Certified Dish, then at once: every certification at the location ends, the Restaurant removes every mark and the decal,
and we take the Restaurant off Vegans Explore (its listing is removed, not deleted). The Restaurant may not apply for certification or be
listed again for **2 years**; after that it may apply again. No fees are refunded.

## 6. Using the mark

6.1 We grant the Restaurant a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to use the mark only as set out in Schedule B,
only beside Certified Dishes that are active on the public status page, and only at the named location.

6.2 Every menu page, board or delivery listing that carries the mark also carries the key: the number of certified items out of the menu
total, what else the Restaurant serves (meat, fish, dairy or eggs), that it is not an allergen claim, and the live status link.

6.3 The mark is never used as a sign for the whole restaurant, on its logo, or in advertising that suggests the Restaurant is Vegan.

6.4 On suspension, expiry, revocation or withdrawal of a dish, the Restaurant removes the mark from that dish everywhere, including delivery
apps, within **[2 business days]**. On the end of this agreement, it removes every mark and the decal in the same time.

## 7. Suspension, termination and appeal (G7)

7.1 We may suspend a Certified Dish at once when a gate is no longer met, whether or not anyone meant it. Intent decides the penalty, not
the suspension.

7.2 Three material events in any 12 months start a termination review. Knowing falsification of evidence or records ends this agreement at
once.

7.3 The Restaurant fixes the cause, shows us how, and may ask for a re-check, charged per Schedule E.

7.4 The Restaurant may appeal a suspension, revocation or termination within 10 business days, in writing. A person who took no part in the
decision hears it and decides within 20 business days after the record is complete. The mark stays off during the appeal.

## 8. The public registry

8.1 We publish the Restaurant's name, location, Certified Dishes, their status (certified or suspended), **the date each dish was last
checked**, the count of certified items, what else it serves and the term, on the public registry, its QR status page and its Vegans Explore
listing.

8.2 We never publish a failure, a reason for a suspension, or the evidence.

## 9. Records (G8) and confidentiality

9.1 The Restaurant keeps its evidence for the Certified Dishes (invoices, specification sheets, recipes, training records) for the term and
5 years after, and shows them to us on request.

9.2 We keep the audit file for the term and 5 years after. We treat the Restaurant's recipes, supplier lists and evidence as confidential and
use them only to run the certification, except where the law requires otherwise.

## 10. Diner reports and tastings

10.1 Diners may report a concern, without giving their name, through the report button on the status page and the Restaurant's listing.
We may investigate any report, including with a secret shopper or a targeted check, and may suspend a dish at once while we do.

10.2 One community tasting night is included with each certification. Invited members taste the Certified Dishes and send honest feedback,
which reaches the Restaurant without their names (our team knows who said what). Tasting scores are feedback only and never decide pass or
fail. The Restaurant provides the tasting portions. Tasters who eat free disclose it when they post.

10.3 The Restaurant keeps its staff trained so that anyone who serves a Certified Dish can say it is 100% plant-based and point the diner to
the status page.

## 11. Independence

11.1 Verifiers are trained contractors, never our employees. Verifiers, desk reviewers and approvers are paid per file, with no bonus for a pass. They accept no free meals, gifts or payment from the
Restaurant, and anyone who has advised the Restaurant in the last **[24 months]** does not work on its file.

## 12. Liability and insurance

12.1 The Restaurant indemnifies us against claims arising from its food, its premises, its staff, or its use of the mark outside this
agreement.

12.2 Our total liability under this agreement is limited to **[the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim]**. Neither party is liable
for indirect or consequential loss.

## Schedules

**A. Certified Dishes.** Name as on the menu, location, the menu total (M), the date each was certified.

**B. Mark usage guide.** The art and rules in `docs/certification/b5-stamp-and-diner-test.md` (seal 1 inch or larger; the small mark only
with the words; the key on the same page; the whole decal only; removal within 2 business days).

**C. Evidence package (E1 to E8, one per gate).** E1 every ingredient by brand and product with its specification or label photo
(distributor letters alone do not count); E2 stocks, doughs, glazes, pan release, oils and shared bases traced; E3 the protected path:
storage, utensils, surfaces, pans, fryer and water, and any shared oven procedure; E4 every menu, board and delivery listing that names the
dish; E5 the designated manager and the training roster; E6 the change procedure; E7 a named contact for access and corrective action; E8
how records are kept. Banned without exception: honey, beeswax, carmine, shellac, gelatin, isinglass, animal rennet. Need product-specific
proof: natural flavors, wine, beer, vinegar, vitamin D3, sugar, enzymes, glycerin, emulsifiers, fermentation media. "Contains milk or egg"
fails; "may contain" is disclosed.

**D. Incident plan.** Each event, who acts, the time limit, and what the status page shows: a changed dish (stop at once, suspended), a
complaint (targeted check within [10 business days]), a recall touching an ingredient (suspended until cleared), a failed spot check
(suspended), falsification (ended).

**E. Fees.** $250 per dish for the first term (desk review, visit, one tasting night, one courtesy check). $150 per dish per renewal. A
changed dish's new review: **[$150]**. A re-check after a suspension: **[$150]**. No free certifications.

**F. The Standard**, version 1.2, as published in our methodology.

**G. Starter kit and print service.** Included: the window decal and the menu marks as files. Optional, paid: we redesign, print and deliver
the Restaurant's menu and decal; nothing goes to print until the Restaurant approves the proof. **[Price after a printer's quote.]**
