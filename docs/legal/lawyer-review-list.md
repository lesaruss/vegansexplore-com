# Lawyer review list (2026-11-09)

Everything Vegans Explore and LESARUSS have drafted that a lawyer should read once, in the order it blocks money or a launch.
All of these were drafted in-house. Ask the reviewer for redlines plus a short note on anything that must change, not a rewrite.
Florida law throughout unless noted. Contracting entity: LESARUSS Inc. (Vegans Explore is its DBA; Vegans Explore Inc. is being dissolved).

## Tier 1: blocks money now

1. **BrandDesk Managed Marketing Agreement**, version 2026-10-31.
   Where: `business/agreement.html` (live at vegansexplore.com/business/agreement).
   Needs a new version for the relocked prices (pricing_v9): BrandDesk Site $500 a quarter, no setup; photos $250; video $250;
   Managed $500 a month as the step up, plus a $250 setup done live on a Hot Lab call.
   **The v9 draft is written** (2026-11-12): `docs/legal/managed-marketing-agreement-v9-draft.md`, every change marked and seven
   questions for counsel at the end (send counsel that file with the live one). The Community Manager's $150 onboarding share is
   internal and is not in the agreement.
   Ask: it has **no limitation of liability or indemnity clause**; is that acceptable? The 30-day notice after 12 months, the
   unpaid-setup-owed-on-early-end term, the 14-day work pause and 30-day termination for non-payment, Pause at $99 a month
   (up to 3 months a year), and "they own the assets" (we assign copyright in what we make once the setup is paid).
   Signed online: full name typed, I agree ticked, time, IP and version kept. Is that enough as a signature?

2. **Passport Challenge stop agreement**, version 2026-11-01.
   Where: `business/challenge.html` (step 3, The agreement).
   A business gives four $25 digital gift cards for a featured month; members bid points in the Vault to win them.
   Ask: promotional value only, $25 off one visit, no cash back, expires 12 months after won, void if the business closes.
   Florida gift card / promotional certificate rules, and whether the points auction (no money, highest points bid wins, one win
   a month) is a contest, a sweepstakes or neither.

3. **Partner plan terms**, $111 every 111 days (Front Row Start), and the general business terms.
   Where: `policies.html#business`.

## Tier 2: before each program launches

4. **Pre-Vegan Certification restaurant agreement** (draft for counsel, with brackets).
   Where: `docs/certification/b6-restaurant-agreement-draft.md`, with its own nine questions for counsel at the end.
   Also read with it: `docs/certification/b7-verifier-manual.md` (Verifiers as paid-per-visit contractors) and

   Ask: a duty to diners from a pass or fail, "certified" and "Vegan" claims (FTC and Florida), the 2-year removal as a remedy.

5. **Contractor agreement for paid gig work** (not drafted yet: needs a template).

   Must include: every shoot assigns copyright to LESARUSS Inc. so we can pass it to the client.

6. **Group Partner and Nonprofit Partner terms** (in `policies.html#membership`).
   Commissions of 20% to 50% paid to a group or nonprofit as referral earnings, never a donation; W-9 at $25; 30-day hold.
   Event tickets: our 5% fee, card processing on the buyer, each group's own refund policy.

7. **Releases**, all short and signed online:
   - Comment requests (`comment.html`): we may quote, play and edit a person's comment.
   - Host stories (Events Guide): voice recordings used on our shows, 18 and over.
   - Member photos from visit bounties and check-ins: license to us and to the restaurant.
   Ask: is one plain release enough, and is ticking a box valid for each?

8. **Brand campaign rules** (grand prize, city top-10 prizes, digital rewards at set point totals).
   Not live yet. Ask: Florida game promotion rules, "no purchase necessary" wording, registration or bonding thresholds.
