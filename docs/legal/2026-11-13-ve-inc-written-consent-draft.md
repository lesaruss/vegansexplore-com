**WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VEGANS EXPLORE INC.**
**IN LIEU OF A MEETING**

Vegans Explore Inc., a Florida not for profit corporation, Sunbiz document number N24000008839, EIN 99-4445734 (the "Corporation").

The undersigned, being all of the directors of the Corporation, act by written consent without a meeting under section 617.0821,
Florida Statutes, and adopt the following resolutions. This consent has the effect of a meeting vote.

**1. Board of directors**

RESOLVED, that the board records that Ella Magers resigned as a director by written notice delivered on October 21, 2026, effective
on delivery (section 617.0807), and that John Thomas is no longer a director; and that the directors of the Corporation are the
undersigned.

**2. Earlier consent replaced**

RESOLVED, that the written consent dated October 8, 2026, approving the renaming of the Corporation to LESARUSS Foundation, Inc., is
withdrawn and will not be carried out.

**3. Dissolution**

RESOLVED, that the Corporation, which has no members entitled to vote on dissolution, be dissolved and its affairs wound up under
sections 617.1402 and 617.1403, Florida Statutes; and that the President is authorized to sign and deliver Articles of Dissolution to
the Florida Department of State once the steps in resolution 4 are complete.

**4. The Vegans Explore business moves to LESARUSS Inc.**

WHEREAS, the services paid for through the Corporation's accounts were provided by LESARUSS Inc., and the Corporation holds no
property other than a small cash balance;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation transfers to LESARUSS Inc. the use of the name "Vegans Explore," its website and online accounts, and
its customer subscriptions, so that LESARUSS Inc. carries on the Vegans Explore business under that name; and that the President is
authorized to move the Corporation's Stripe account and other accounts to LESARUSS Inc. and to sign any document needed to do so.

**5. Plan of distribution of assets** (section 617.1406)

RESOLVED, that the following plan of distribution of assets is adopted:
(a) All liabilities and obligations of the Corporation are paid and discharged, or adequate provision is made for them.
(b) The Corporation holds no assets on a condition requiring their return, transfer or conveyance on dissolution.
(c) Any assets received and held subject to a limitation permitting their use only for charitable, educational or similar purposes
    are transferred to **Animal Hero Kids**, an organization engaged in Vegan and humane education, activities substantially similar
    to the Corporation's.
(d) The articles of incorporation and bylaws do not provide for distribution to members or others.
(e) All remaining assets are also distributed to Animal Hero Kids, so that the Corporation's entire remaining cash balance goes to
    Animal Hero Kids.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that an officer of the Corporation authenticates a copy of this plan, certifies that it was adopted under section
617.1406(2), and files it with the Florida Department of State.

**6. Closing the Corporation's tax accounts**

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to file Form 966 and the Corporation's final federal and state returns, to request
deactivation of the Corporation's EIN once all returns are filed and taxes paid, and to close the Corporation's bank account after
the final distribution.

**Effective date:** when the last director signs and delivers this consent to the Corporation.
