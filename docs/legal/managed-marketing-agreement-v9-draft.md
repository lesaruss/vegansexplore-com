## BrandDesk Agreement

Version 2026-11-12 · Vegans Explore, a brand of LESARUSS Inc.

### 1. Who this is between
This agreement is between LESARUSS Inc., 300 SW 1st Ave, Suite 155, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301, doing business as Vegans Explore
("we", "us"), and the business named when you sign ("you").
It starts on the day you sign and your first payment goes through.

### 2. Your plan **[changed]**
You pick one plan when you sign. Your plan is shown on your checkout, in the email we send you, and on your business dashboard.

**Site.**
- Your website on our template, fed by your Vegans Explore Directory page.
- Your logo and brand kit: your colors, type, voice, story and menu copy, each part approved by you.
- Your Directory page and business dashboard set up, and Vegans Explore member sign-in on your website.
- A Community Manager who gets you set up: coordinating your photos (ours or your own uploads) and answering your questions.
- A heads-up when a content creator plans to visit, and the content they make sent to you.
- A check-in with your Community Manager every 3 months.
- A place in a group Hot Lab, a group help session, when you want one.

**Managed.** Everything in Site, and:
- Setup done with you live, in one Hot Lab call: your Google Business Profile, Search Console and social accounts connected in one
  session. We aim to finish everything we control within two weeks of that call. Google's own verification can take longer; it
  shows as waiting until Google finishes.
- Google kept current: hours, photos, posts and replies to reviews.
- A monthly check-in with your Community Manager on a set list of questions.
- One campaign a month, picked with you from our list of ideas.
- Your results on your business dashboard.
- Social media (if you add it): two social platforms managed for you.

**Add-ons, on either plan, if you pick them.**
- Photos: up to 8 dishes, shot to one standard. A larger shoot is quoted separately.
- Video: a narrated slideshow of your photos.

### 3. What it costs **[changed]**
| Plan or add-on | Price | When |
|---|---|---|
| Site | $500 every 3 months | At signing, then every 3 months on the same day |
| Managed | $500 a month | At signing, then each month on the same day |
| Managed setup | $250, once | At signing |
| Social media, two platforms (Managed only) | $500 a month | With your Managed payment |
| Photos (up to 8 dishes) | $250, once | At signing, if you add it |
| Video | $250, once | At signing, if you add it |

Site has no setup fee. Payments go through Stripe to the card or account you give at checkout. Prices do not include any sales tax
that applies. Your prices stay as signed for your first 12 months (Managed) or your first 4 periods of 3 months (Site).

### 4. How long it runs **[changed]**
- **Site** runs 3 months at a time and renews on its own. Either of us can end it by notice in writing before the next 3 months start;
  it then ends at the end of the 3 months already paid for.
- **Managed**: the first term is 12 months from the day you sign. After that it continues month to month, and either of us can end it
  with 30 days' notice in writing.

### 5. Ending it early **[changed]**
- **Site**: see section 4. Nothing more is owed.
- **Managed**: you can end it in the first 12 months by telling us in writing at contact@lesaruss.com. The service ends at the end of
  the month you have already paid for. [Nothing more is owed: the setup is paid at signing. Counsel: should an early end in the first
  12 months owe anything more?]

### 6. Pausing **[changed]**
Managed only. You can pause for up to 3 months in any 12 months, for $99 a month. A pause starts on your next billing date and ends
on its own. While paused, your website and your Library stay up, and we stop managing. Site does not pause; it ends or renews every
3 months.

### 7. What is yours **[changed: the unlock]**
You rent the service. You own the assets.
- Always yours: your domain, your Google Business Profile (you are the primary owner; we are a manager on it), your social media
  accounts, and your Directory listing, which stays free.
- Made for you, and yours: the photos and videos, your logo in every format, your brand kit, graphics and menu boards, and the copy we
  write for you. Every photographer we hire assigns their copyright to us so we can pass it to you. Full-size files of what we make
  are yours once it is paid for. [2026-10-31 said "once your setup is paid in full"; in v9 there are no installments.]
- Your Library: every file on your business dashboard, to download one at a time or all at once as a ZIP, at any time.
- Your asset sheet: all your copy, your menu with descriptions and prices (also as a spreadsheet), hours, links, colors, fonts and
  your Google details, kept current.
- Your old website: we archive a copy of your current website when we start, so nothing is lost.
- The website template itself, its design and code, stays ours. Your content in it is yours.

### 8. When it ends (unchanged)
However it ends, we send you the ZIP of your Library and your asset sheet right away, and offer a handoff call. You have 30 days to
download anything else. Then the Library closes and the website on our template comes down. Your domain is yours to point wherever
you choose.

### 9. What we need from you (unchanged, plus one line)
- Accurate information about your business, and word from you when it changes.
- Manager access to your Google profile, your domain settings and your social accounts, as each job needs.
- Your approval on each part of your brand kit.
- The food for shoots.
- Replies within a few business days. When we are waiting on you, timelines move by the same amount.
- **[new]** On Managed: time for the setup Hot Lab call, with the logins for the accounts we connect.

### 10. Visits, photos and people (unchanged)
Creators, members and your Community Manager visit to photograph and post about your place. When a visit is comped, the post says
so, as the FTC requires. Your customers are photographed only with their consent.

### 11. What we do not promise (unchanged)
We work hard to grow your business, but we cannot promise sales, ratings or search rankings. Being a client never buys a higher
place in the Vegans Explore Directory: your page can wear your brand, and the order still comes from member votes.

### 12. Payments that fail **[changed: the reference]**
If a payment fails, Stripe tries again and we let you know. If a payment is still unpaid 14 days after it was due, we pause our work
(your website stays up) until it is paid. After 30 days unpaid, we may end the agreement under section 5.

### 13. Limits on what either of us owes **[new: proposal for counsel]**
[Neither of us is liable to the other for lost profits or indirect damages. Our total liability under this agreement is limited to
what you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. You are responsible for the information, menus, prices and claims you give us
to publish, and you will cover us if a third party claims they are wrong (for example, an allergen or a "Vegan" claim). We are
responsible for the content we create, and will cover you if a third party claims it infringes their rights.]

### 14. The fine print (unchanged, renumbered)
After your first 12 months, we give you 30 days' notice of any price change. This agreement is governed by the laws of Florida. It
is the whole agreement between us about BrandDesk; a change needs both of us in writing (email counts).

### 15. Signing **[changed: the name and version]**
You sign at checkout by typing your full name and ticking "I agree to the BrandDesk Agreement". We keep a record of your name, the
date and time, this version (2026-11-12), your plan and the address you signed from, and send you a copy by email.
