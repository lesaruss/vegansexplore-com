# ve-theme-park-guide: Magic Kingdom draft list (2026-11-04)

Made by hand from three pages read today: **V** = VeggL, "Updated 2025" (veggl.com, says it was pulled from Walt Disney World's Plant Based Eating Guide); **P** = PETA (peta.org/living/food/vegan-at-disney-world, undated, written after Disney's 2019 launch); **N** = VegNews (vegnews.com/vegan-guides/vegan-guide-disney-world, undated, mentions the 2019 launch). Facts only: restaurant, item name, who lists it. No wording or photos copied.

**Important:** all three say they got it from Disney's own plant-based guide, so "two sources agree" means two copies of Disney, not two checks. Nothing here is confirmed in the park. Every item enters our Guide as **Ask before you order**, with its source link and date, until a member confirms it at the restaurant (first-to-confirm bounty).
