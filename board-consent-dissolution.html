Written consent of the board of directors of Vegans Explore Inc. in lieu of a meeting

Vegans Explore Inc., a Florida not for profit corporation, Sunbiz document number N24000008839 (the "Corporation"). The undersigned, being all of the directors of the Corporation, act by written consent without a meeting under section 617.0821, Florida Statutes, and adopt the following resolutions. This consent has the effect of a meeting vote.

  1. Board of directors. The board records that Ella Magers resigned as a director by written notice delivered on October 21, 2026, effective on delivery (section 617.0807), and that John Thomas is no longer a director; and that the directors of the Corporation are the undersigned.
  2. Earlier consent replaced. The written consent dated October 8, 2026, approving the renaming of the Corporation to LESARUSS Foundation, Inc., is withdrawn and will not be carried out.
  3. Dissolution. The Corporation, which has no members entitled to vote on dissolution, is dissolved and its affairs wound up under sections 617.1402 and 617.1403, Florida Statutes. The President is authorized to sign and deliver Articles of Dissolution to the Florida Department of State once the steps in resolution 4 are complete.
  4. The Vegans Explore business moves to LESARUSS Inc. Whereas the services paid for through the Corporation's accounts were provided by LESARUSS Inc., and the Corporation holds no property other than a small cash balance: the Corporation transfers to LESARUSS Inc. the use of the name "Vegans Explore," its website and online accounts, and its customer subscriptions, so that LESARUSS Inc. carries on the Vegans Explore business under that name. The President is authorized to move the Corporation's Stripe account and other accounts to LESARUSS Inc. and to sign any document needed to do so.
  5. Plan of distribution of assets (section 617.1406). (a) All liabilities and obligations of the Corporation are paid and discharged, or adequate provision is made for them. (b) The Corporation holds no assets on a condition requiring their return, transfer or conveyance on dissolution. (c) The Corporation holds no assets received subject to a limitation permitting their use only for charitable, educational or similar purposes; the payments it received were for services (podcast hosting, vendor booths, subscriptions), including payments once labeled "donation" in error. (d) The articles of incorporation and bylaws do not provide for distribution to members or others. (e) All remaining assets are distributed to LESARUSS Inc., which provided the services the Corporation was paid for. An officer of the Corporation authenticates a copy of this plan, certifies that it was adopted under section 617.1406(2), and files it with the Florida Department of State.
  6. Closing the Corporation's tax accounts. The officers are authorized to file Form 966 and the Corporation's final federal and state returns, to request deactivation of the Corporation's EIN once all returns are filed and taxes paid, and to close the Corporation's bank account after the final distribution.

Effective date: when the last director signs and delivers this consent to the Corporation.
