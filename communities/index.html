Grant program

Mission-driven orgs run free

If your organization exists to serve animals, communities, or the plant-based world, we want to support you. Every application is reviewed by our team - we look at alignment, not just paperwork.

Submit your application

Tell us about your organization. If you have a 501(c)3 certificate, upload it. Every application starts here regardless of status.

Share your story

Optionally, share a link to a short video explaining your mission and why VE should sponsor your community. This helps our team understand your work.

Our team reviews

We look at mission alignment, community fit, and long-term partnership potential. Holding a 501(c)3 does not guarantee approval - we review every applicant the same way.

Payment link issued (if approved)

Approved organizations receive a payment link for either a discounted rate or, where granted, donated service. We reach out directly with next steps.

Who can apply

Nonprofits, advocacy orgs, and mission-aligned communities

We believe the most impactful communities in the vegan world shouldn't be priced out of the tools they need to grow. The grant program exists to change that.
