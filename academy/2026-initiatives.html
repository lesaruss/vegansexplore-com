2026 Initiatives Portfolio

The 13 programs Sean laid out for 2026, straight from him. This is the real answer to "what does the year look like." Not all 13 activate at once, and several are still being workshopped, not locked. 1. Vegan Tailgate / Sports Nights Concept

A recurring sports-night series built on Sean's existing relationships with the Marlins, the Heat, the Dolphins, and an NHL team, plus college sports. The model is group sales: a sponsor funds a vegan chef or vendor, influencers attend, tickets get sold, and turnout is what proves the model to the next team, keeping financial risk low for the host org. This has real track record already: the 2021 Heat tailgate sold out and led to the Heat bringing in their first permanent vegan vendor a year later; the 2021 Dolphins tailgate proof-of-concept also sold out.

Every event is filmed and produced for YouTube, and partner orgs share it for extra reach. Open items: which team/city runs the next pilot, and locking in a sponsor and vendor for it.

2. Supermarket Sponsorship Campaigns Concept

Precedent from Sean's Toonies days with NutritionSmart and Vegan Fine Foods: an in-store event, a prominent speaker, food demos, free and open to the community. Influencers shoot content that promotes the store's vegan section, producing a content series pushed on both VE's and the store's feed. Every chain gets a custom pitch, not a template. Target deal size: $2,000 to $10,000+ per market, and the same chain relationship can become several regional campaigns since VE operates in multiple regions.

Open items: choosing the first target chain to approach and building that chain's custom pitch.

3. Monthly Badge / Rewards Challenge Concept

Styled on Chipotle's "Summer Extras" map. Members visit select restaurants, drawn from $111/mo Business Membership partners, throughout the month to collect badges that convert to points and giveaway entries. Free to join, Passport holders get bonus points. The restaurant set rotates every month. This is one of two real use cases for reactivating VE's dormant points/leaderboard system, the other being points-banking for Business Membership partners.

Open items: the badge/map UI still needs to be built, and the first month's restaurant set hasn't been selected yet.

4. End of Year Gala Concept

An annual event, rotating across VE's operating regions, celebrating the year's wins in the vegan movement. Award categories, like Vegan of the Year and others, are nominated and voted on by VE members themselves, not picked internally, with a deliberate focus on spotlighting under-recognized organizations and people. It's a dual-purpose fundraiser, for VE and for the partner organizations helping stage it.

Open items: picking the first host city and defining the award categories and voting process.

5. World Vegan Expo Concept, IP Secured

The flagship. There's a real gap here: Plant Based World Expo is drifting from a strict plant-based focus, and VE has already secured the "World Vegan Expo" IP. The vision is a World's Fair revival, run FIFA/World-Cup style, one flagship event a year with a rotating global host city, cities competing to host it. Tracks: animal rights, food technology, sustainability, and lifestyle, with influencers treated as real partners, not just invited.

Near term: a small kickoff/announcement event is targeted for November 1, 2026, to put the market on notice ahead of the full expo landing sometime in 2027.

6. VEGANS EXPLORE Community Night Proven Model

The marquee, monthly event in every city VE operates: speakers, panels, dinner, vendors, giveaways, and it doubles as a recruitment tool for people who don't know VE yet. This is exactly how SoFlo Vegans grew from 2 people to 20 to 50, consistently, monthly, in the same location. The scale goal behind this initiative is VE's long-term target of 1,000 simultaneous locations.

Open item: formalizing a standard monthly run-of-show template beyond the SoFlo Vegans precedent, and scheduling the rollout to new cities.

7. Plant Based Showcase Concept

Can piggyback onto existing non-vegan events, not always standalone. The best local chefs are sponsored to make roughly 250 samples, with a voting competition for attendees' favorite chef. Monetization is flexible: ticket sales, buy-in from a host event, a flat fee covering chef and VE costs, one big sponsor, or several sponsors each backing an individual chef. Chefs get same-day video content, a podcast spot, good photos, and real paid compensation, and their recipes become browsable on VE's recipe platform.

Open items: confirming the first chef(s) and choosing the monetization model for the pilot.

8. Movie Night Concept

Partners with new documentary releases for a coordinated multi-city tour: director/producer Q&As, local vendor snacks, a host, and content capture. The pitch to filmmakers is direct: VE hands them a finished event plus an existing community and existing theater relationships. Also extends to general premieres, screenings, and vegan-adjacent mainstream films. Worth checking whether the International Vegan Film Festival still exists, and filling that gap if not.

Open items: confirming that status, and identifying the first filmmaker partner and pilot city.

9. Partner Communities Still Workshopping

White-labels VE's own city-community model (South Florida, Central Florida, New York) for outside organizations, animal rescue and advocacy groups, who want an official community space inside VE's ecosystem. For 501(c)(3) partners, it doubles as a fundraiser: anyone signing up through their link becomes a VE member, and the org gets a cut, Sean is floating something like 50/50 on an $11 one-time fee, structured so it could eventually offset what they pay VE monthly.

Sean flagged this one himself as needing real workshopping, including directly with Community Managers, before it locks. Nothing here is final.

10. Vegan Business Certification Program Concept, Real Lead

Modeled directly on kosher certification: a checklist, a live inspection, a report of what to fix, re-inspection, certification once everything checks off, and annual recertification, plus ongoing secret-shopper audits through the year. Certified businesses get "christened" official plus a real marketing campaign, both an event and a digital push. The strategic goal is packaging this as an easy pitch to major food distributors, Sysco and Cheney Brothers named specifically.

There's a real, already-had lead here: Sean previously had an actual conversation with Cheney Brothers about becoming their plant-based consultant across their 1,000+ business accounts, worth revisiting once a pilot has real proof points.

11. B2B Vegan Product Sourcing Directory Real Pilot Partner

A standardized marketplace/directory of vegan product suppliers so coffee shops, juice bars, and caterers can source and order in one place, with VE acting as the middleman, similar to drop-shipping. It's bundled into the $111/mo Business Membership rather than sold separately; a per-transaction fee is still just a maybe, not decided. Vegan Corner (Miami) has already expressed real interest in piloting this.

12. Vegan Tours Concept

Full-day Sprinter-van excursions: an outdoor activity, goodie bags, music, an influencer hired as host, and a dedicated video person capturing content, aimed at locals and especially tourists. Booked at least a month in advance. Community Partner businesses can claim a stop on the route, getting business sent their way in exchange for making people feel special and contributing to goodie bags. Part of the broader strategy to pitch tourism bureaus and municipalities directly, positioning VE as a strategic city-activation partner, not just an event organizer.

13. Guided Marketing Consultation / Campaign Management Concept

Sits underneath everything else: VE sits down with an organization, builds their campaign, keeps it organized, and project-manages it for them, "DIY but with a hand held." Roughly $1,500 per campaign at standard pricing, with the first few organizations piloted at a lower cost until there are solid case studies to point to.

Rollout Strategy and Business Membership

Not all 13 activate at once. The plan is to pilot one initiative per city as the prototype, learn what works, then push it to other cities, alternating different initiatives across different cities in a steady cadence rather than launching everywhere simultaneously. The point is to give investors and sponsors something concrete enough to feel real, not hypothetical, and several of these already have real track record behind them (SoFlo Vegans' growth, the Toonies-era supermarket events, the Cheney Brothers conversation, Vegan Corner's interest).

The $111/mo Business Membership underlies several of these: entry into VE's resource directory with access to VE's other managed brands, subsidized managed marketing and web development via the LESARUSS AI partnership with real scheduled consultations, points banked from the monthly fee (not dollar-for-dollar) redeemable toward those services and bankable before ready to use, first and inside access to promotional opportunities, a dedicated check-in contact, and a growing resource library from VE's partner network.

None of this is locked. Several pieces, Partner Communities' revenue split and the B2B directory's transaction fee especially, are still being workshopped. Full detail on each initiative, including budget, run of show, stakeholders, and open items, lives in that initiative's own Campaign Bible.
