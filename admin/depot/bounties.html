Bounties

Campaigns, claims, questions and work for bounties (playbook: Bounty Lifecycle). Members see campaign cards at vegansexplore.com/bounties, soonest deadline first; a campaign shows only while it has jobs. Any leadership member can propose a campaign. The city's Community Manager decides. Review within 5 days: Approve at full points or fewer (say why), ask for Changes (2 rounds, 72 hours each), or Decline. A coaching note is optional and goes to the member either way.

Strikes

1 strike: 30 days without claiming. 2 in 6 months: 90 days. 3, or a violation: paused until cleared. A release after claims close is half a strike. Clear one when a member explains what happened.
