Referral claims

When someone joins, the window asks Did someone send you? (Sean, 2026-11-03). The link always wins, so credit is never taken from a sponsor by a typed name. A Conflict is a name that disagrees with the link. Not found is a name we could not match to anyone. You decide. Changing who is credited works only before the friend joins, since the 111 points and any commission are paid when the account turns active. After that, the decision is recorded and you give the points by hand from the member card.
