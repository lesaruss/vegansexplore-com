Business badges

Each business's own intake answers set its badge (100% Vegan, 100% Plant-based or Pre-Vegan). We never judge it for them. 100% Vegan shows only after you check it; until then their page shows 100% Plant-based. Open one to read their answers, then approve 100% Vegan or note that the team will follow up. Pre-Vegan Certified never comes from answers, only from our item checks.
