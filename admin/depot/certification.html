Pre-Vegan Certification

The dish registry. Each certificate is one location for one 12-month term, and each named dish on it has its own status. Only Certified and Suspended dishes show on the public status page (the QR); Applied and In review never show, and no reason ever leaves this page. A dish becomes Certified only when you approve it, and you cannot be its Verifier or desk reviewer. Every change needs a reason and is kept for the term plus 5 years.
