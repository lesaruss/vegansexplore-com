Passport Challenge onboarding kit

What each Passport Challenge stop gets alongside its register card (printed from Depot > Passport Challenge). Pick a piece, then print. The staff sheet prints on US Letter; the sticker is a 6 inch circle for the front window.

VEGANS EXPLORE

We're a Passport Challenge stop

Each month, Vegans Explore Passport members visit a set of Vegan and Vegan-friendly businesses in one part of the city and collect a stamp at each one. This month, that includes us. Every stamp is a real guest who bought something here.

When a guest mentions the Passport Challenge

  1. They buy anything, as usual.
  2. They show you the Passport Challenge on their phone. Hand them the Passport Challenge card from under the register.
  3. They scan the QR code on the front. The stamp lands on their phone right away.
  4. Take the card back and put it under the register.

What to say

"Doing the Passport Challenge? Scan this after your purchase."

Good to know

Do I need to track anything?No. Each guest can only stamp once per business per challenge, and it is all on their phone.
Do they need to buy something?Yes. The card comes out after a purchase. Who gets the card is the business's call.
The card is missing or damaged?Email hello@vegansexplore.com. We send a new card, and the old code stops working.
A guest says it did not work?They can tap "The cashier didn't know?" in the app. We call the owner to confirm, and their stamp still counts.

Keep the card out of sight so it only goes to paying guests. A new card comes with each monthly challenge. Questions: hello@vegansexplore.com
