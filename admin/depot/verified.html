Passport Partners

Every Passport Stop ($250 a quarter) and Passport Anchor ($500 a quarter) business (Business Offer v2). Businesses join from vegansexplore.com/business, /claim or their own listing page and renew every quarter through Stripe. Before a city's Passport Challenge launches they reserve a founding spot for free; open their billing here once their first Challenge month is set. The Verified badge goes on the listing once you mark the visit done (Verified means we visited; it can't be bought). Anchor businesses sit first in their hub section from the day they pay.
