Passport Challenge

Each month, one area of a city. Set up the challenge, add the stops, print each stop's register card, and confirm "cashier didn't know" reports. Members see it at vegansexplore.com/passport/challenge once its city is launched, while its status is Live and today is inside its dates.

Launch

Planned for 2027, one city at a time. Until a city is switched on, members there see "Coming in 2027" and its challenge stays a preview for Community Managers and superadmins (listed on their dashboard).

Stops

Each stop gets its register card (Print card on its row) plus the onboarding kit: the staff sheet, the window sticker and the staff video script.
