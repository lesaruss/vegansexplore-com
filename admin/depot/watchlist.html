The Depot

Watchlist

Accounts that may be gaming points. The system only gathers evidence and watches quietly; nothing changes for a member until you decide. Open one to see why it was flagged, their points, who is around them and the history, then clear them, void their points, suspend them (points and taking part are frozen, and you can reverse it) or ban them (you can reinstate them later). Every decision needs a note and is logged.
