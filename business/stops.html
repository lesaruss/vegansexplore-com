How the Passport Challenge works

  1. A map of one neighborhood.Each month, Passport members get a map of one part of the city with a pin for every stop.
  2. They come to you and buy.A member buys anything, and your cashier hands over the Passport Challenge card from under the register.
  3. Stamped at your register.They scan the card and the stamp lands in their Passport. Five stamps earn the month's badge and Points.
  4. You see who came.Every quarter you get a results sheet: members who stamped with you, first-time visitors and Tour guests.

We're choosing the first businesses on the map

The Passport Challenge launches in 2027, one city at a time. Founding businesses reserve a spot now and pay nothing until their first Challenge month starts, and the founding price stays locked as long as they stay. Spots in each city are limited to the stops on its first map.
