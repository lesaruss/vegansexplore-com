With or without a sponsor

Your stores stay. A sponsorship puts our members to work on them.

Without a sponsorship
  • Your store pages stay in the Guide
  • Members update them as they happen to shop, the organic way
  • Updates earn the standard, smaller rewards
With a sponsorship
  • We run bounties across your stores: map the aisles, find your weekly Vegan specials, shoot photos and short videos in store
  • Bigger rewards, so updates come in fast and stay current
  • Your specials in the Daily Pulse, your stores featured, ads on your pages

Yours to keep: once your stores are in the Guide, they stay after a sponsorship ends. What ends is the bounties and the featured placement, and updates go back to the organic pace.
