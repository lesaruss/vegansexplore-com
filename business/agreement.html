Managed Marketing Agreement

Version 2026-10-31-draft · Vegans Explore, a brand of LESARUSS Inc.

Draft for legal review. This is written from the terms we have set and is not yet approved by counsel. Until it is, checkout runs in test mode and no card is charged. The yellow notes are open questions, each with the answer we wrote in for now.

1. Who this is between

This agreement is between LESARUSS Inc., 300 SW 1st Ave, Suite 155, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301, doing business as Vegans Explore ("we", "us"), and the business named when you sign ("you").

It starts on the day you sign and your first payment goes through.

2. What we do

Setup. We aim to finish everything we control within two weeks of your welcome call:

  • A full audit of how your business shows up online.
  • A logo and brand kit: your colors, type, voice, story and menu copy, each part approved by you.
  • Your Google Business Profile claimed and completed. Google's own verification can take longer; it shows as waiting until Google finishes.
  • Your website on our restaurant template, fed by your Vegans Explore Directory page.
  • Your Directory page and business dashboard set up.
  • A starter photo shoot of up to 15 menu items, all shot the same way.

Every month.

  • Your managed website. The template keeps improving, and every improvement reaches your site.
  • Google kept current: hours, photos, posts and replies to reviews.
  • A monthly check-in with your Community Manager on a set list of questions.
  • One campaign a month, picked with you from our list of ideas.
  • A photo night every two months after launch: a few dishes shot during a creator night at your place. You provide the food.
  • Your results on your business dashboard.
  • The ad console with a capped allowance, included from the day it opens.

Social media (if you add it). Two social platforms managed for you.

3. What it costs

$1,500Setup, once. Paid at signing, or in 3 monthly payments of $500, or 6 monthly payments of $250.
$500 a monthThe managed service, from the day you sign.
$500 a monthSocial media, two platforms, if you add it.

Your first month and your setup (or its first payment) are charged when you sign. After that, each charge is on the same day of the month. Payments go through Stripe to the card or account you give at checkout. Prices do not include any sales tax that applies.

Your prices stay as signed for your first 12 months.

4. How long it runs

The first term is 12 months from the day you sign. After that it continues month to month, and either of us can end it with 30 days' notice in writing.

Open question: the notice period after the first 12 months. Written in: 30 days.

5. Ending it early

You can end the agreement in the first 12 months by telling us in writing at contact@lesaruss.com. The service ends at the end of the month you have already paid for. Any part of the setup fee not yet paid becomes due.

Open question: whether the monthly fees for the rest of the first 12 months are owed on an early end. Our terms say only that the rest of the setup is owed. Written in: they are not owed.

6. Pausing

You can pause for up to 3 months in any 12 months, for $99 a month. A pause starts on your next billing date and ends on its own. While paused, your website and your Library stay up, and we stop managing. You can pause once your setup is paid in full.

7. What is yours

You rent the service. You own the assets.

  • Always yours: your domain, your Google Business Profile (you are the primary owner; we are a manager on it), your social media accounts, and your Directory listing, which stays free.
  • Made for you, and yours: the photos and videos, your logo in every format, your brand kit, graphics and menu boards, and the copy we write for you. Every photographer we hire assigns their copyright to us so we can pass it to you. Full-size files unlock once your setup is paid in full.
  • Your Library: every file on your business dashboard, to download one at a time or all at once as a ZIP, at any time.
  • Your asset sheet: all your copy, your menu with descriptions and prices (also as a spreadsheet), hours, links, colors, fonts and your Google details, kept current.
  • Your old website: we archive a copy of your current website when we start, so nothing is lost.

The website template itself, its design and code, stays ours. Your content in it is yours.

8. When it ends

However it ends, we send you the ZIP of your Library and your asset sheet right away, and offer a handoff call. You have 30 days to download anything else. Then the Library closes and the website on our template comes down. Your domain is yours to point wherever you choose.

9. What we need from you

  • Accurate information about your business, and word from you when it changes.
  • Manager access to your Google profile, your domain settings and your social accounts, as each job needs.
  • Your approval on each part of your brand kit.
  • The food for shoots and photo nights.
  • Replies within a few business days. When we are waiting on you, timelines move by the same amount.

10. Visits, photos and people

Creators, members and your Community Manager visit to photograph and post about your place. When a visit is comped, the post says so, as the FTC requires. Your customers are photographed only with their consent.

11. What we do not promise

We work hard to grow your business, but we cannot promise sales, ratings or search rankings. Being a client never buys a higher place in the Vegans Explore Directory: your page can wear your brand, and the order still comes from member votes.

12. Payments that fail

If a payment fails, Stripe tries again and we let you know. If a payment is still unpaid 14 days after it was due, we pause our work (your website stays up) until it is paid. After 30 days unpaid, we may end the agreement as an early end under section 5.

Open question: the 14 and 30 days. Written in as our default.

13. The fine print

After your first 12 months, we give you 30 days' notice of any price change. This agreement is governed by the laws of Florida. It is the whole agreement between us about managed marketing; a change needs both of us in writing (email counts).

For counsel: limits on liability, indemnity, how this ties to our Privacy Policy (/policies#privacy), and any clauses needed for a business that is outside Florida.

14. Signing

You sign at checkout by typing your full name and ticking "I agree to the Managed Marketing Agreement". We keep a record of your name, the date and time, this version (2026-10-31-draft) and the address you signed from, and send you a copy by email.
