Opening January 1, 2027

A presence in every city where we have a chapter

From January 1, 2027, we will be in every chapter city through our members, community bounties, local partners and daily content, and on the road at events around the world.

  • Our membersPeople who live there, checking in, voting and sharing the places they love.
  • Community bountiesSmall jobs that send members out to find, photograph and confirm what is new.
  • Daily contentThe Daily Pulse, our social channels and the Vegans Explore Podcast every week.

These are our plans for 2027, not results. We report real numbers as they come in.
