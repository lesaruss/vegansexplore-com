Our story

It started in a locker room.

Two sprinters went plant-based in their junior year and spent the next two seasons hunting for a protein bar that didn't taste like chalk or fall apart in a gym bag. They never found one, so after graduating they started making their own in a shared commercial kitchen.

The first batch was peanut butter, made with pea protein and roasted pumpkin seeds. Their old teammates ate the whole run in a week. Every product since has had to pass one test: does it hold up to real training?
