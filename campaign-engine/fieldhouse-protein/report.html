The moment that made it work

Week 6: money moved to what was working

Week6

Atlanta's run clubs were turning into sales at twice the rate.

The live dashboard showed Atlanta's run-club check-ins turning into retailer clicks at twice the rate of the other two cities, while DMV ad-rail clicks were the lowest of any line. Because the budget was managed, we moved $3,000 out of the DMV ad rail into two extra Atlanta creator posts and a run-club bounty. Fieldhouse saw the change, and the reason, on its dashboard the same day. Atlanta finished with the most retailer clicks of any city.

Atlanta's retailer clicks per week rose from 300 in week 4 to 470 in week 7, while the DMV held steady on its creators and members alone. On a self-directed plan, Fieldhouse would have seen the same numbers and made the call itself. On managed, we made it and showed the reasoning on the dashboard.

Ask
Show how plant protein fits your training, in your own words and your own routine.
Deliverables
One collab video (45 to 90 seconds) and three short clips over ten weeks.
Pay
$1,200 for the video and $500 per clip ($2,700), plus a $300 bonus at 400 retailer clicks on your tracked link. Extra posts: $1,000 each.
Must
Label every post #ad. Use your personal tracked link. Show the product being used, not just held.
Never
Make health or weight-loss claims, or say the product replaces a meal.
Rights
Fieldhouse can use the videos on its profile and social channels for 12 months, credited to you.

How a creator gets a campaign like this

  1. Join as a creator in your city's community.
  2. Claim a spot when a sponsor's creator bounty opens.
  3. Get the brief, your tracked link and a sampler box.
  4. Deliver and get reviewed, with notes if something needs a fix.
  5. Get paid per piece, plus bonuses for results.

Payment runs through the same bounty system members use, so every creator sees their work, approval status and pay in one place.
