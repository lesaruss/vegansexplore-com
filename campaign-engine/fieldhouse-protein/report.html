The moment that made it work

Week6

Atlanta's run clubs were converting at twice the rate

The live dashboard showed Atlanta's run-club check-ins turning into retailer clicks at twice the rate of the other two cities, while DMV ad-rail clicks were the lowest of any line. Because the budget was managed, we moved $3,000 out of the DMV ad rail and into two extra Atlanta creator posts and a run-club bounty. Fieldhouse saw the change and the reason on its dashboard the same day. Atlanta finished with the most retailer clicks of any city.

Why managed paid for itself

Week6

$3,000 moved from the DMV ad rail to Atlanta

Atlanta's retailer clicks per week rose from 300 in week 4 to 470 in week 7 after the move, while the DMV held steady on its creators and members alone. On a self-directed plan, Fieldhouse would have seen the same numbers and made the call itself. On managed, we made it and showed our reasoning on the dashboard.

See it on the dashboard

Six creators, two per city

Each creator got the same brief, a sampler box, and a fixed rate per piece, with a bonus for hitting a retailer-click goal on their tracked link. All six hit it. After week 6, both Atlanta creators were paid for one extra post each.

The brief

Ask
Show how plant protein fits your training, in your own words and your own routine.
Deliverables
One collab video (45 to 90 seconds) and three short clips over ten weeks.
Pay
$1,200 for the collab video and $500 per short clip ($2,700), plus a $300 bonus at 400 retailer clicks on your tracked link. Extra posts: $1,000 each.
Must
Label every post #ad. Use your personal tracked link. Show the product being used, not just held.
Never
Make health or weight-loss claims, or say the product replaces a meal.
Rights
Fieldhouse can use the videos on its Featured Profile and social channels for 12 months, credited to you.
