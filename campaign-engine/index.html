The Campaign Engine

We don't sell reach. We give everyone the same engine to run on: members who take part, local creators, events in every city, and a report showing where every dollar went.

The more people who walk through the door, the more it works for everyone.

How it works

One loop. It gets stronger with every city.

Each part feeds the next. None of it works alone, and all of it works better when more people take part.

  1. 1Members get toolsThe Directory, the Passport, the Daily Pulse and city events make vegan life easier.
  2. 2That builds an audienceActive members in every city we serve, around the things they actually use.
  3. 3Brands and events joinBrands, sponsors and hosts put their budgets into campaigns on the engine.
  4. 4Campaigns become paid workMembers earn points, creators get paid, hosts get sponsored events.
  5. 5More members joinEvery campaign brings new members, so the next brand starts bigger.

We are building this together. Season 1 is when we work it out, then we keep adding people and cities.
