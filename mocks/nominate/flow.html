1 · Nominate someone

Give someone their flowers, and turn it into their way in

Anyone nominates someone from their community. We review it. If we agree, Kai invites them to an interview that is also their membership survey. They finish as members, take a short dashboard tour, and the best stories get featured in their community and on the show.

Built on what we already runThe SoFlo show's Nominate someone (/comment/soflo-nominate) and the Pulse's Monday Who should we interview? already collect nominations. This makes it one system for every city, with the nominee's email and a real next step.
It is an audition, said plainlyKai makes them feel picked, because they were. Their answers are being considered for a feature, and nothing is promised. Every screen says "considered", never "featured".
The interview is the doorThe survey is the only way in. The interview carries the Explorer questions, so finishing it makes them a member and earns the Explorer badge, no second survey.

The four steps

  • 1 Who their name, their email (so we can reach them), their city, and what they are: a person, a business, a nonprofit, an activist. A business name searches the Directory as you type.
  • 2 Why them record, speak or write. Voice is encouraged, never required.
  • 3 You your name and email, and "Can we say you nominated them?" Signed-in members skip this step.
  • 4 Send read it back, the release box ("you may play or read my words"), Send.

After Send

  • Thank you screen with Nominate someone else and Share. The nominee hears nothing until we approve.

How it sends

  • One template a BrandDesk Outreach campaign you approve once. Each Approve and invite in the Depot sends it to that one person.

11 · When we pick a story

Their voice, on their city's page and the show

The city hubThe Community Voices card plays their answer in their own voice, "Nominated by Dana P." under it, and a link to their profile.
The podcastThe city show's Local voice segment: the nomination, then their origin story and one or two answers, cut from the recordings. Typed answers are read by a Guide.
The Daily Pulse and socialA Pulse story the week it airs, and a social card from their interview, with the nominator tagged if they said yes.
We tell them first"Your story was picked" email before anything goes live, with the date. Never a surprise.
Not picked yetA warm note: your interview stays with us, and you are still a member with everything that comes with it.
The nominatorHears when their person is featured, with the link to share.
12 · Your calls, with my defaults

Say go, or change any of these

1. One system, not a new one. Build /nominate on the comment requests we already run (ve-comments, the recorder in /comment), adding the nominee's email and city. SoFlo's button points to it.Default: yes.
2. The interview is their way in. It carries the Explorer questions, so finishing makes them a member with the Explorer badge, no second review (we already approved them).Default: yes, plus a new Nominee badge.
3. Points for the nominator. A nominator who is a member gets 111 points when their nominee finishes the interview, the same as a referral.Default: yes, 111 points, members only.
4. Kai's words. The overview line, eight questions and the confirmation line are drafts on slides 6, 7 and 9. Text only, no recording.Default: none ship until you approve the wording.
5. The dashboard tour is for everyone. One tour, walked by Liz, for every new member from any door, built before the front door opens.Default: Liz, six stops. Script to you first.
6. Self-nominations. SoFlo's form allows them today.Default: allowed, marked Self-nominated in the queue.
Build order: the dashboard tour first (it serves every door), then the nomination form, the Depot queue, the invitation, Kai's interview and the city hub section.
