The rule

  • Every city show is a Vegans Explore show. Its name is the community's short name plus Podcast: SoFlo Vegans Podcast for South Florida, CenFlo Vegans Podcast for Central Florida, and so on as each community gets one.
  • No show gets its own logo. The suitcase mark signs the cover; the picture is that community's backdrop, the same art as its community page.
  • The show's name is on everything we post for it: the cover, the thumbnail, the share image and the last carousel slide.
  • It is one more way for the community to get the content we already make.

10 · End card · where to listen, link in bio

Colors by kind of story

  • Directory green: a business or new listing.
  • Events orange: an event, its date as the picture until it has its own image (none this week).
  • Pink: a person, a Guide or a commercial.
  • Pulse green: a bounty or a news item.
  • Brand green: the show itself, cover and close.
  • No buttons or QR codes on feed pictures: nothing in a post can be tapped. The end card names where to listen and points to the link in bio; real links ride the story sticker, the caption on Facebook and YouTube, and the email. QR codes are for print and big screens only.

Never changes

  • The frames, the stripe, the suitcase mark, Montserrat, the orange button, the logo files.
  • Words never on a picture; every picture made in the shape of its spot; no face ever cut off or covered.
  • The day plan: Tue teaser, Wed carousel and reel, Thu the local voice, Fri the listings, Sat the events (this week the Holiday Guide), Sun the bounty.
  • Only who you clear is featured or contacted; someone mentioned on the show is not promoted by default.
  • Render scripts in scripts/brand-templates/ fill these parts from the episode, so a pack is a run, not a design session.
