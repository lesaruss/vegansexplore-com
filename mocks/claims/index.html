How it works

  • Every campaign we run becomes a bounty: one row in our database, aimed at the businesses it fits. It shows on every dashboard it fits at once, and in the weekly email. No code for a new one.
  • They pick what fits them. A gift card bounty is good at any of their locations; a chain does it once.
  • Points land when our team confirms it's done, as member bounties work today. Points are given, never bought.
  • Points are the only way a business posts its own bounty for members: a new dish to try, content to make. So they take part in our campaigns to earn them.
  • Member bounties open in a community once its Community Manager is on board. Later, on paid campaigns, we add to their bounty.
