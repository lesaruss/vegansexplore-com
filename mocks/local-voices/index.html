What happens here

  • Not in the Directory: we make the listing (or a People page for a person) with our usual checks: name, place, links, logo copied in, Vegan status.
  • Listing ready: it is live and the tile links to it. Only then do we draft anything to them.
  • Needs you: if we can't confirm something (is it Vegan? which location?), it waits for you instead of guessing.

What I'd build, and in what order

Your calls
1One campaign for every showWith a Show filter, rather than a campaign per show. Default: yes.
2Outside sources start with podcast feedsA short list of Vegan shows' RSS feeds you pick; news articles come through the web reader we already use. Default: you pick 5 shows to start.
3Asked onceSomeone mentioned again next week is not asked again; anyone who answered or said no stays closed. Default: yes.
4One ask, two partsThe claim (their free page) and their story (the podcast) in the same message. Default: yes.

Build order

  • 1. The six tabs on Local Voices, Sunni and Carla moved in.
  • 2. Our shows feed Queued when an episode's notes are published.
  • 3. Listing ready on the tile, then drafts.
  • 4. Approve one or many; emails scheduled, DMs ready to send.
  • 5. The Sent list with clicks, replies and stories.
  • 6. Outside sources into Review (podcast feeds, then news).
