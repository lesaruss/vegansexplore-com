Rules

  • Headlines are 900, tight (-.025em), line height about 1.04. Sizes flex between the desktop and phone numbers.
  • Body copy is 600, never lighter than 400, and keeps its own reading width (about 860px) while the page stays full width.
  • Capitals with spacing only for eyebrows, tags, buttons and the word under a big number.
  • Numbers are the loudest thing in a block: live counts from data, never a typed chapter or city count.
  • No second font, no italics for emphasis (use the marker), no em-dashes, "Vegan" always capitalized.

  • The band runs from 56% to 92% of the line, so it sits under the lower half of the letters and the words stay ink.
  • It wraps cleanly onto a second line (box-decoration-break: clone), with a 4px corner.
  • Only on headings 26px and up: pages, section heads, social, thumbnails, video titles.
  • On dark green the marker word turns bright green instead of getting a band (as in "Your vote moves the list").
  • Never peach or mint (too faint), never on a face or a logo.

Rules

  • Outline 2px ink, corner 14px, band 46 to 54px in the section color.
  • The logo sits on a white square over the band edge and always links to the profile.
  • Title and summary clamp to two rows. One action row, same place on every card.
  • Shadow 6px right, 6px down, solid, no blur; on hover the card lifts 2px and the shadow grows to 8px.
  • Peach and mint sections use an ink shadow (their own color disappears on white).
  • Blocks (doors, features) are solid color with no outline; the picture sits in a white frame.

The real file, every time. Never a drawn copy.

Clear space on every side: the height of the suitcase handle. Smallest size: portrait 64px wide on screen, landscape 120px. Never over a face; over a picture it sits on a solid plate or uses the all-white file. The inverted file is for black and dark colors that are not green; brand green takes the all-white file.

Locked

Foundations are locked. Sean went with all four.

1The pink shade

A, Blossom #FF9EC4, with deep pink #C2185B only when pink has to be text on white. It is already the concept you locked, and it supports orange instead of competing with it.

2Section colors

The map on slide 3: Directory brand green, Events orange, Guides pink, Communities mid green, Pulse bright green, Media peach, Passport mint. Directory doors keep their own colors inside.

3Orange is the only "do this" color

Yes: one orange button per view, everything else white or green. It keeps the call to action obvious on every page and every post.

4The stripe on everything we make

Yes: once per page, along the bottom of every social post and thumbnail, across the top of every email, under every lower third. It is what makes a frame read as ours at a glance.
