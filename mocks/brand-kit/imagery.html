Hand-drawn people, real places, Vegan food you can almost taste.

4 · Shapes

Five shapes, each drawn for its spot.

1:1 · squareMid shot, head to waistAvatars, the Guide picker, tiles, phones.
4:5 · sceneA Guide doing the page's thingGuide pages, scene cards, vertical video.
9:16 · tallFull bodyStories and reels only.
16:10 · wideBanners and door blocksDirectory doors, Guide cards, thumbnails.
21:9 · strip NewThin strips on a pageDrawn for the strip: every face in the middle band with room above, so a page can trim it to 3:1 without touching a head.
  • Every spot names its shape, and its picture is made in that shape (1856 x 2304 for 4:5; saved JPEG, at most 1920 px wide).
  • Ask for a Guide's picture with VEGuideArt(slug, 'square'|'tall'); never point a page at the file.
  • Never squeeze a picture into a frame it was not drawn for: no 16:10 forced into a strip, no full body in a square.

7 · Real photos and other brands' logos

Photos for real places and people only.

When a photo is right

  • A real listing's own place, food and team (their profile, their card).
  • A real person: a guest, a member, Sean on the podcast. Approved creators and team also get a LESARUSS-style avatar.
  • Real events we were at.
  • Never a stock photo standing in for an idea.

How photos are handled

  • Pictures from other sites go through /_vercel/image?url=&w=&q=75 (hosts listed in vercel.json).
  • Repo photos: JPEG, at most 1920 px wide, about 80 quality; banners under about 500 KB.
  • In a card or block, a photo takes the same white frame and corners as our art.

Other brands' logos

  • Always copied into vegan-media storage, never hot-linked.
  • Shown on a white square, contained, never cropped or recolored.
  • Every logo links to its profile, /directory/<slug>.
  • A logo over a picture sits on a solid plate, never on a face.
