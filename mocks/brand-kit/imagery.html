1 · Our illustration style

Hand-drawn people, real places, Vegan food you can almost taste.

Clean ink lines, soft painted colorConfident outlines, warm light, gentle texture. Never flat vector, never 3D, never a photo.
Everyone doing somethingShopping, cooking, wrapping, adopting. A scene shows the page's action, not a pose.
Real adults, real rangeAbout seven and a half heads tall, every age, skin tone, body and ability. Dani is always in her wheelchair.
The food is the heroColorful, plant-based plates up front and in focus whenever food is in the story.
Our mark, no one else'sGuides wear the Vegans Explore suitcase mark. No other logos, no readable words in a scene.
Warm, lived-in placesMarkets, kitchens, cafes, parks, string lights. Real places drawn, never generic stock rooms.

7 · Real photos and other brands' logos

Photos for real places and people only.

When a photo is right

  • A real listing's own place, food and team (their profile, their card).
  • A real person: a creator, a guest, a member, Sean on the podcast.
  • Real events we were at.
  • Never a stock photo standing in for an idea.

How photos are handled

  • Pictures from other sites go through /_vercel/image?url=&w=&q=75 (hosts listed in vercel.json).
  • Repo photos: JPEG, at most 1920 px wide, about 80 quality; banners under about 500 KB.
  • In a card or block, a photo takes the same white frame and corners as our art.

Other brands' logos

  • Always copied into vegan-media storage, never hot-linked.
  • Shown on a white square, contained, never cropped or recolored.
  • Every logo links to its profile, /directory/<slug>.
  • A logo over a picture sits on a solid plate, never on a face.

Your default, applied

GeekFon keeps its own style

GeekFon art is its own brand's look. It appears on Vegans Explore only as itself (a featured artist, an episode), credited, in a white frame, and goes in the kit as that example, not as a second Vegans Explore style.

A Vegans Explore Guide is never redrawn in the GeekFon style, and a GeekFon character is never put in a Vegans Explore tee.

The line between them

The one shared object is Sean's GeekFon wearable, because he really wears it. If you meant something else by "the GeekFon image," say so and this slide changes.

Your call

Four calls to lock Imagery. My default is in bold.

1The style, do and don't, as written

Lock slides 2 to 8 as the imagery standard. They restate rules you already set (scene recipe, mid shot in squares, nothing over faces, drawn not photoreal) in one place.

Yes: replace welcome-ensemble.jpg on the five pages that still use it (the Welcome Guide, Nominate, the business Guide, Taste, one mock) with the approved group shot, cropped square. It is what made the Guides look short.

Yes: new scenes dress the Guides in brand colors (pink, cream, black, brand green, orange), the suitcase mark always visible, Liz in her pink tee often, so the art lands on the Color Block page without clashing.
