1 · Header and words

Every view opens the same way: tag, headline, line

Example For brands · December 1 to 11

Give our members a holiday pack

One line under the headline says what the view is for, in plain words.

The parts
  • Eyebrow tag .k-eb: a solid dark green tag (.or orange, .pk pink), never loose green letters.
  • Headline .k-h1 / .k-h2: Montserrat 900, sentence case, tight.
  • Marker .k-hl.g/.o/.p: one or two key phrases per headline.
  • Line .k-lede, labels .k-label, the stripe .k-stripe once per page.
  • Never all-caps headlines, gray thin titles or a green word with no marker.

The rules
  • Orange is the one thing to do on the view. In a walkthrough the slide bar's Next is that button.
  • Every other button is white with an ink outline; dark green only for an on state.
  • 6px corners on every button, tab and field, never pills.
  • Status is a tag in plain words, never an action word. Sample stamps any demo.
  • Never a green filled button as the main action, never two orange buttons in one view.

The parts
  • .k-rows > .k-row: a colored square (.k-num.or/.pk/.br/.pe/.ti/.dk), the title and one line, then one tag or one button.
  • .k-steps: dates and how-it-works; .you marks the reader's part.
  • Short lists show five, then Show N more opens the rest in place. Nothing scrolls inside itself.
  • Lists of cards fill the width: .k-grid, four across on desktop.
5 · Forms and tabs

Thick ink fields, the orange send, one step per view

BackStep 2 of 4
The parts
  • .k-form > .k-f (.wide spans both columns): ink 3px fields, an orange focus shadow.
  • Dropdowns: no browser arrow, the chevron at 16px from the edge (.k-select).
  • More than one section: one step per view, .k-stepbar with Back, Step N of M, Next; ?step= in the address.
  • .k-tabs: one sliding row (dashboards); outside a dashboard, more than 5 tabs become a dropdown.
  • Every text field gets the speak mic by itself (ve-dictate.js).

How it runs
  • node scripts/look-check.js checks every page changed against main.
  • It runs by itself before every push from a build session (.claude/settings.json); a failure stops the push and says why.
