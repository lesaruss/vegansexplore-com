10 · Why suggestions dry up, and the fix

The sweep only looks where you rarely find them

Category (live)No logoWebsite on fileInstagram on fileFacebook on file

The sweep has looked 1,971 times: 1,209 logos used, 755 found nothing, 5 turned down.

Today

  • The sweep reads only the listing's website and its Instagram. It never looks at Facebook or Google, where you find them fastest.
  • The rescues, sanctuaries, studios and venues came from Google searches, so they have a website and nothing else on file. Their site often has no clean logo, so the sweep gives up.

The fix, in order

  • 1 Read the socials linked from their own website and save them (free, already built for Add a listing).
  • 2 Add their Facebook page picture as a candidate (through Apify, a fraction of a cent a page).
  • 3 Add their Google listing's photos as candidates, the same photos you see on Google (counts against your $50 Google cap; about $7 per 1,000).
  • 4 Re-run the sweep on the 755 that found nothing, most viewed first.

What retiring means

  • The page leaves the menu and the home. Its file, its data and its server function stay exactly as they are.

Why it's real timeIt isn't a copy that has to sync. Both doors open the same app and the same database, so a change made in one is there the moment you open or refresh the other. There's only one BrandDesk to keep current.
How it's servedvegansexplore.com passes the BrandDesk pages through from HQ (the way lesaruss.ai already shows our Directory). It opens on the Vegans Explore desk, with our menu around it.
Signing inYou sign in once on Vegans Explore. A small bridge gives you an HQ session for the same account, so BrandDesk knows it's you. Only super admins and people you add to a brand get in.
What moves off the DepotSocial, Playbooks, Ideas, Analytics and everything under People (slide 8). Each moves only after BrandDesk has everything that page does.
Two gaps to close firstAnalytics: Insights needs the Communities ranking, Directory, Events, Media, Guide tabs and Google costs. Outreach: the one-to-one messages and the people sender need a tab in BrandDesk > People.
Not iframing HQI'm not putting HQ in a frame: Safari blocks sign-in inside a frame from another site, so you'd be signed out on your phone.
