How it works

  • A business or organization claims its page (free for members). From then on, signed in, its own page shows Your dashboard. Visitors never see it.
  • It opens at /dashboard/business, where every member feature lives, and each part is its own view in the address bar (?tab=opportunities), so Back works and Share sends it. Coming
  • One template for every kind of listing: a restaurant, a brand, a group, a nonprofit. The parts are the same; what fills them changes by kind (slide 8).

What changes from today

  • Today the business dashboard lives on the listing page (?tab=brand). Live It moves into the dashboard, on the same shell as the creator dashboard, so every dashboard reads as one family.

9 · The template

One shell for every dashboard, locked once you approve

The parts, in this order (one dropdown, more than five)
1Homeneeds you, new for you, your week
2Your pagethe editor and the menu
3Media kitfeatures, story, links, brand library
4Opportunitiescontribute, feature, sponsor, ads
5Messagesour team, in the platform
6Insights7, 30, 90 days or a year
7Your planmembership, Partner, BrandDesk

What gets locked

  • The shell: the creator dashboard's top bar (square picture with rounded corners, eyebrow, breadcrumb, the parts dropdown), the same for businesses, organizations and creators.
  • One view per part, in the address bar (/dashboard/business?tab=...); a list, then one item on its own.
  • Rows: every row the same fields, one button in the same place, status as a colored tag in plain words.
  • Feeds, not code: opportunities, features and messages are rows in the database, so a new one reaches everyone it fits without a release.
  • Phones first: everything stacks, the dropdown fills the width.

Build order, once you approve

  • 1. The shell and Home. 2. Media kit (the Holiday Guide features fill it first). 3. Opportunities. 4. The weekly email. 5. Messages. Push notifications come with the app.
