How it works

  1. Visit any stop on the map and buy anything.
  2. Ask for the Passport Challenge card at the register.
  3. Scan the QR on the card with your phone camera. Your stamp lands right away.
  4. One stamp per business. Reach the goal before the month ends for the badge and Points.
