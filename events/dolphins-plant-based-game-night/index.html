Vegans Explore Is Going to a Dolphins Game.

The first plant-based day at a Miami Dolphins home game at Hard Rock Stadium. Vendors, sampling, and community, all free to walk through. We're finalizing the exact date with the Dolphins now, the plan is locked, this is happening.

What This Is

A plant-based day built into a Dolphins home game, ten years of South Florida plant-based community work landing in one of the biggest rooms in the state. No tickets required to walk through, meet the vendors, and sample what's new, part of what we've been building toward since our first activation at Hard Rock Stadium in 2021.

Vendor Village

Curated plant-based food, wellness, and lifestyle vendors from our South Florida network. Come taste, sample, and meet the people behind the brands.

Free Community Members

Vegans Explore members get first access to the group ticket link once it's live, at member pricing, before it opens to the general public.

Built for a Purpose

A portion of ticket proceeds supports Vegans Explore's nonprofit work, keeping South Florida's plant-based community growing for the next ten years.

Why We're Doing This

We ran the first Dolphins plant-based tailgate in 2021 and it sold out. That same year our Miami Heat activation sold out too, and a year later the Heat brought on their first permanent plant-based vendor as a direct result. One night becomes a lasting relationship, that's the pattern, and this is the next one.

Ten years, hundreds of activations, nine cities and counting. This is the biggest room South Florida's plant-based community has stepped into yet.
