Vegans Explore Inc. is the Florida nonprofit behind Vegans Explore. Sean is reorganizing it, and as a director your approval is part of making that official. You can approve, step down from the board, or ask to talk first. Any of the three is fine.

What is changing

  • The nonprofit becomes LESARUSS Foundation, Inc. Same organization, new name. It will raise money and give grants to animal rescues, Vegan community groups and K through 12 education initiatives.
  • Vegans Explore becomes a brand of LESARUSS Inc. The membership, Guides, Directory and events run as a regular business, so the people who work events can be paid.
  • The Foundation registers with Florida before it ever asks the public for money.
  • Nothing is asked of you beyond this. No money, no fundraising, no ongoing duties, unless you would like to stay involved.

Written consent of the directors of Vegans Explore Inc. in place of a meeting

The undersigned, being all of the directors of Vegans Explore Inc., a Florida not-for-profit corporation (Sunbiz document N24000008839), take the following actions by written consent under s. 617.0821, Florida Statutes. Each action is effective when the last director signs.

  1. New name. The Articles of Incorporation are amended to change the corporation's name to LESARUSS Foundation, Inc. The President is authorized to sign and file the Articles of Amendment.
  2. Brand sale. The corporation sells the name and brand "Vegans Explore" to LESARUSS Inc. under the Brand Sale Agreement, for $111, which the directors find fair to the corporation: the brand was built mainly with Sean A. Russell's own money and unpaid work, and the corporation spent little of its own funds on it. The corporation keeps its cash and all money already received.
  3. Conflict of interest. Sean A. Russell disclosed that he owns LESARUSS Inc. The directors other than him approve action 2 knowing that interest.
  4. Florida registration. The corporation files the full charity registration with the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services under Chapter 496, Florida Statutes.
  5. Board size. The directors elect Sean A. Russell and Claudia Russell as directors, to the extent either is not one already, so the board has the three directors Florida requires.
  6. Past acts confirmed. The directors confirm the acts taken in the corporation's name by its officers since it was formed, including its annual reports and the Vegans Explore memberships sold.
  7. Signing. The officers may sign and file anything needed to carry out these actions.
