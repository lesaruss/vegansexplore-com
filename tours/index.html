Vegans Explore Tours

Once a month, a van of up to 12 of us rides together to three Vegan tastings in one neighborhood, then finishes at a local attraction. A host is on board the whole way. $50 a guest covers the ride and every tasting.

  1. Pick a tour and book your seats, up to the whole van. Tell us about diets and allergies.
  2. Meet at the pickup spot. We ride together between stops.
  3. Three tastings, then the attraction, then back to the drop-off.
