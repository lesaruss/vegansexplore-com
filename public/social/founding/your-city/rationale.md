# Rationale: Your city (place and belonging)

**Strategy in one line:** make the reader find their own city first, then show them what is already theirs there, so Founding Member reads as "be one of my city's first," not "sign up for an app."

**Why a stranger stops:** slide 1 is a wall of city names on brand green with no picture to scroll past. People scan any list for their own name; the moment they find their city (or a city they love), the post is about them. Pink highlights on Atlanta, Seattle and London show the reach from the South to the West Coast to Europe.

**How each slide moves them:**
1. Find your city (self-recognition, the hook).
2. Atlanta: your city has a hub (belonging, local pride, our drawn city art at full 16:9, never cropped to a square).
3. River at a night market: the people you meet (community, not features).
4. Seattle: every Vegan spot near you, vote and save (the everyday use).
5. A real Los Angeles listing: Check in +10 (proof it is live, and points are a reward loop).
6. London: nominate someone, record your story (local pride, a role to play).
7. Theo: free Guides for every step (welcome to Pre-Vegans too).
8. The Founding Member card: stays on your profile for good, closes midnight Eastern January 1 (status plus scarcity).
9. End: four cities, "Your city is counting now.", one orange button.

**Urgency:** a fixed, stated deadline, a permanent badge that disappears after it, and the framing that each city's founders are being counted now (local competition and pride), with zero cost as the removed objection.

**What I would test:** the cover's highlighted cities (rotate per audience or ad set, e.g. a South Florida version); "Find your city" vs "Is your city on here?" on the cover; slide 3 scene vs a real chapter event photo once we have one; end button "Join free: link in bio" vs "Claim your city's spot".

**Requests (not faked):** a square Atlanta and London art piece would let slides 2 and 6 run the city full-bleed; a real chapter meetup photo would make slide 3 stronger social proof.
