# Rationale (scarcity-identity)

**Strategy:** Founding Member is a title you can only claim now and keep for good; the deadline is on screen from the first slide to the last, and every middle slide shows one real thing membership does for you.

**Why a stranger stops:** the cover is not a screenshot; it is a membership card with a "Closes Jan 1" stamp and the line "This title ends January 1." A title with an end date reads as something you could lose (loss aversion) and as a club of early people (belonging).

**How each slide moves them:** 1 the title and the clock. 2 it stays on your profile for good (identity). 3 to 8 one concrete use each, in plain words: food near you, points for going, your city (every chapter named, which Sean asked for), events, Guides, free shows. 9 the loss made visible: the same person joining now vs after Jan 1, side by side. 10 the end card: free, a short survey, the exact deadline, link in bio, one orange button.

**The visible clock:** a dark green rail beside every picture with a "Jan 1, Midnight Eastern" calendar and a meter that loses one bar per swipe, so the reader literally watches the window empty as they go. It sits on color, so pictures stay whole (4:5 art shown 4:5, no face cut).

**What I would test:** cover A (card) vs B (Liz in a group, liz-fnd-picnic); the meter vs a plain calendar; "Free shows" vs "Give points to causes" on slide 8; end card city South Florida vs the viewer's top city by audience data.
