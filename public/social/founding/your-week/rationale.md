# Rationale: Your week

**Strategy in one line:** tell it as the viewer's own week, one day per slide, so every feature arrives as a moment in their life instead of a screenshot.

**Why a stranger stops:** the cover is a feeling, not a product: "Monday. 6:47 PM. Nothing to eat." Every Vegan has stood there. It opens a loop (what happens next?) and the seven-day row plus "Swipe through your week" promises a short, finite story.

**How each slide moves them:** a day rail on the left of every scene (day, time, seven week squares filling in) is the progress bar; the viewer wants to finish the week. Slide 2 closes the cover's loop one minute later (6:48 PM, Near me). Then a small win (check in, points), something to enjoy (a free show), being the friend with the plan (saved places), going out (an event), helping (giving points to a cause). Slide 8 answers "is this in my city?" with every chapter by name.

**Urgency:** slide 9 turns the story: the week is free forever, Founding Member is not. A calendar page reads JAN 1, 11:59 PM ET. The end card repeats the deadline beside the one orange button.

**Scenes** are 4:5 Liz scenes drawn whole at 864 x 1080 beside the rail, never cropped or stretched. Renderer: `render.js` (reads the kit's CSS).

**What I would test:** cover hook (6:47 PM line against a scene cover of Liz with her phone); the rail on or off; the order of Sunday (giving) and the cities slide; "Join free" against "Join free by Jan 1" on the button.
