Leadership

Community Manager

You run VEGANS EXPLORE in your city. Building the local community, showing up at events, connecting with Vegan businesses, and developing the people around you. It starts volunteer. It pays $4,000 a month when your city hits its milestone - no renegotiation, automatic.

What the Role Looks Like

Day to day you find and onboard Vegan businesses, recruit and support Vegan Explorers, show up at events, and make sure your region is actually building. You're the first person a new member meets and the last line of quality for everything that comes out of your city.

This is a leadership role, not a content creator role. The job is building something real - a local community that keeps growing after you leave a room.

The Pay Path
Volunteer + Affiliate→$2K / mo Stipend→$4K / mo Salaried

You earn affiliate commission from day one - on every member and Community Partner you bring in. Hit the first milestone and the $2,000 monthly stipend kicks in. Hit the full milestone and you're at $4,000 with quarterly bonus pool access. Everything is defined upfront.

Explorer

Vegan Explorer

You already know your city's Vegan scene - where to eat, what's worth trying, what events actually show up. The Vegan Explorer program puts that to use. You explore, document, and review. Your work goes directly into the live directory, credited to your profile, visible to the whole community.

What You Contribute

You find and submit Vegan businesses, events, and spots in your city. Every verified listing earns points. You write honest reviews and keep your city's section accurate. The quality of your work is the quality of the platform in your region.

What You Get Back
  • Points toward your membership - every verified listing and review earns.
  • Your name on your work. Every contribution is permanently credited to your profile.
  • Featured placement in the directory if you're a top contributor in your region.
  • First consideration for Community Manager openings in your city.
