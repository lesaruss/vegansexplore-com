        How it works. Pick a campaign, then a job. Each job has a few spots and a clock. Claim a spot before claims close (48 hours before the event) and you get the rundown: what we need, how to shoot it, and the event details, plus a Q&A with your Community Manager until the event starts. Upload your work here (up to 2 GB a file) or paste links, and submit within 7 days of the event. Your Community Manager reviews it; approved work pays the points, and you may get a note to fix something or a tip for next time. Bounties are for Passport holders.
