        How it works. Evergreen bounties are always open: check in where you go, volunteer with a nonprofit, or tell us whether a quiet place is still open. Guide bounties unlock with the Guide you own. For an event job, each job has a few spots and a clock: claim a spot before claims close (48 hours before the event) and you get the rundown: what we need, how to shoot it, and the event details, plus a Q&A with your Community Manager until the event starts. Upload your work here (up to 2 GB a file) or paste links, and submit within 7 days of the event. Your Community Manager reviews it; approved work pays the points, and you may get a note to fix something or a tip for next time. A paid job pays a stipend on top of the points and can ask for a level. Bounties are for Passport holders.
