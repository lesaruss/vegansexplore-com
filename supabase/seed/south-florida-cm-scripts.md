# South Florida Community Manager page: recording scripts

Nine clips, one per slide, in page order. Record each as its own file.
Name each file with its number so I can match it: `01-welcome`, `02-have`, and so on.

**Recording notes**
- Voice only. No music. I put a different music bed under each slide.
- Leave about one second of silence at the start and end of each clip.
- Export as WAV or MP3 (44.1 or 48 kHz is fine).
- Each runs about 20 to 40 seconds. Talk like you're talking to Ron.

## 01-welcome
*Slide: "South Florida is ready for you."*

Hey, and welcome. For more than ten years, I've been building the Vegan community here in South Florida. It's big, it's diverse, and it's not always easy. The goal was always to make it a global hotspot for Veganism. Now I'm looking for someone I trust to carry it forward, from Palm Beach to Miami-Dade. Take a few minutes, and see if that's you.

## 02-have
*Slide: "You get what I never had."*

When I started, there was no playbook. I learned by trial and error, event by event, year by year. You don't have to. You're stepping into a whole team, an ecosystem and a method we've proven. Big events. A platform, in person and online, that supports individuals, nonprofit organizations, community organizations and businesses. And the heart of it hasn't changed. I came in through the food, like a lot of people do. Food opens the door. The animals are the reason we're here.

## 03-lead
*Slide: "Lead the charge. Don't carry it alone."*

Here's why this role matters. The economy has hit our community hard, and Vegan adoption has shifted fast, for a lot of reasons. Your job is to help South Florida grow again, so we can create events, build resources and bring this community back to life. And leading doesn't mean doing it all yourself. It means rallying people. Enroll members and volunteers. Their support powers our points system, the app that's coming, and everything after that. Build something that lasts longer than any one event, or any one person.

## 04-role
*Slide: "Boots on the ground in South Florida."*

So what does the job look like? You're our boots on the ground in South Florida. You tell people what we're building. You show up at events and run our booth. You support local nonprofits and community organizations. You enroll members and bring businesses on as partners. You lead South Florida's conversations on our platform. And you're our eyes and ears in the community. You're not alone, either. My team and I bring the technology, the platform, the guidance and more than ten years of best practices, and we take on the larger partnerships. You lead locally. We've got your back.

## 05-month
*Slide: "What a month looks like."*

So what does a month actually look like? About three hours a week, rarely more than five. Every week, there's the huddle, one group call where every city shares what's happening, plus answering messages from members and local businesses. Every month, you show up to South Florida's events, or send someone from your team, run our booth, and capture photos, contacts and leads. In between, you lead our conversations, invite local businesses, welcome new members and volunteers, and keep up with local news. All of that is covered by your stipend. And if you manage an event, or get assigned to a campaign, that comes with its own additional stipend.

## 06-pulse
*Slide: "Be the first to know."*

Here's something I really need from you. When something happens in South Florida's Vegan community, I want us to hear it from you first. A restaurant opening or closing. A Vegan spot starting to add animal products. The conversations and the controversies people are talking about. You bring it to the huddle. Every week, each city goes around the room: what's happening, what you're up against, and what you want to try. We celebrate the cities that are growing, and we rally around the ones that need support. That's how we build this together.

## 07-season
*Slide: "Here's what a season looks like."*

Here's what our season looks like this fall. We launch with a Community Night in West Palm Beach on Saturday, October 24th. Then Miami on November 21st. On Sunday, November 29th, we're taking the community to the Dolphins versus the Jets at Hard Rock Stadium. And we close out the year in Fort Lauderdale on December 12th. We move around the region so every night feels fresh, from Palm Beach to Miami-Dade.

## 08-agreement
*Slide: "Volunteering, with a stipend."*

Let's talk about the agreement, straight up. This isn't a part-time job. It's volunteering, with a stipend, because you go a little above and beyond what a volunteer does. We start with a three-month trial, so we can both see if it's a fit. Your stipend is two hundred and fifty dollars a month, and it covers your regular role. It starts once your membership goes through, and it's paid each month on the date you registered. On top of that, there are additional stipends when you manage an event or get assigned to a campaign, and as your region grows. We'll be right there with you.

## 09-decision
*Slide: "Ready to lead South Florida?"*

So, are you in? If you'd like to lead South Florida with us, it starts the same way it does for every member. Become a Founding Member. It's eleven dollars, one time. Then confirm you're in, and your certification will be waiting in your dashboard. And if you have any questions before you decide, send them to us right here. Either way, I'm glad you're considering this.
