## 01-welcome
*Slide: "South Florida is ready for you."*

Hey, welcome. If you're hearing this, you care about South Florida's Vegan community, and you want to see it thrive. So do I. I've spent more than ten years building it, from Palm Beach to Miami-Dade. It's big, it's diverse, and it's not always easy. My goal has always been to make it a global hotspot for Veganism. Now I'm looking for someone I trust to lead it with me. Over the next few minutes, I'll be straight with you. What the job is, the time it takes, the stipend, and how to say yes.

## 02-have
*Slide: "You get what I never had."*

Maybe you're wondering if you'd have to figure this out alone. You won't. When I started, there was no playbook. I learned by trial and error, event by event. Since 2016, we've hosted and backed more than five hundred of them. Here's what they taught us. Nobody comes to Veganism the same way. So we meet people where they are, through health, food, nutrition and the environment. But the goal never changes. Animal liberation. A world where no animal suffers or dies for food or entertainment. That takes people with open hearts and open minds.

## 03-lead
*Slide: "Lead the charge. Don't carry it alone."*

Here's why this matters right now. The economy has hit our community hard, and Vegan adoption has shifted fast. That's exactly when a community needs someone to rally it. You could start a Vegan meetup on your own. Plenty of people try. But it gets hard fast when one person does everything. Leading here means bringing people with you. Every member and volunteer you bring in powers our points system and the app that's coming. That's how you build something that outlasts any one event, or any one person.

## 04-role
*Slide: "Boots on the ground in South Florida."*

So, the job. You're our boots on the ground in South Florida, and it comes down to three things. First, show up. Run our booth at events, and support local nonprofits and community groups. Second, bring people in. Tell them what we're building, enroll members, and bring businesses on as partners. Third, lead the conversation. South Florida's members have their own space on our platform, and you're the host. My team brings the technology, the guidance and everything we've learned, and we take on the larger partnerships. You lead locally. We've got your back.

## 05-month
*Slide: "What a month looks like."*

Now, the time. It's about three hours a week, rarely more than five. Every week, there's the huddle, one call with every city, and you answer messages from members and local businesses. Every month, you're at South Florida's events, running our booth and meeting people. In between, you welcome new members and volunteers, invite local businesses in, and keep up with local news. It fits around a real life. That's by design.

## 06-pulse
*Slide: "Be the first to know."*

This one matters a lot to me. Be our eyes and ears. When something happens in our Vegan community, I want to hear it from you first. A place opening, or closing. A Vegan spot starting to add animal products. Whatever people are arguing about. Just tell our assistant on your dashboard. It turns that into stories for our Pulse, and you give them a final look. No code, just conversation. Then bring it to the huddle. When a city grows, we all celebrate. When a city stalls, we all rally. If it's still not working, we talk honestly, and sometimes the role changes hands.

## 07-season
*Slide: "Here's what a season looks like."*

Picture your first season. We open with a Community Night in West Palm Beach on Saturday, October 24th. Then Miami, Saturday, November 21st. On Sunday, November 29th, we're taking the community to the Dolphins versus the Jets at Hard Rock Stadium. And we close the year in Fort Lauderdale, Saturday, December 12th. Four dates, and you'd be at the center of every one, welcoming the people you brought in.

## 08-agreement
*Slide: "Volunteering, with a stipend."*

Let's talk about the agreement, straight up. This isn't a part-time job. It's volunteering, with a stipend, because you'll go a little above and beyond what a volunteer does. We start with a three-month trial, so we can both see if it's a fit. The stipend is two hundred and fifty dollars a month, and it covers the regular role. Manage an event, or get assigned to a campaign, and there's an additional stipend. And more comes as your region grows.

## 09-decision
*Slide: "Ready to lead South Florida?"*

So, here's my ask. Become a Founding Member. It's eleven dollars, one time, the same way every member starts. Your stipend starts once your membership goes through. It's paid after each month, on the date you registered, so your first one comes a month in. Then confirm you're in, and your certification will be waiting in your dashboard. Got a question first? Send it right here on this page. South Florida's Vegan community needs someone to lead it. I'd love for that to be you.
