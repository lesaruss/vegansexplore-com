Good to know

Questions partners ask.

How does checkout work?

Choose an option, add your details, and you go to a secure Stripe checkout. Stripe emails your receipt, we email a confirmation of what is included, and the team follows up with next steps.

What if I sign up later in the season?

The price stays the same; your start date sets how long the package runs. Sign before Oct 24 and a Goodie Bag or Activation Partner package covers every event this quarter. Sign in November and it covers the events that are left.

Can I serve or sell food at my table?

Community Night food is sampling only, unless your business holds the right temporary food event permit. At the Dolphins game there are no sales on the grounds, and bags cannot go into the stadium, which is why bag pickup happens at the November Community Night.

Is a partnership a donation?

No. These are marketing partnerships with Vegans Explore: you get a table, placement in goodie bags, an activation or a custom program in return.

My city is not live yet. Can I still get involved?

Yes. Pick your city in the guide and leave your email. Each city opens as its Community Manager comes on and the season can support it, and the team will reach out when yours does.
