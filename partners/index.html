Common questions

How does checkout work?

Pick an option and pay through secure Stripe checkout. Stripe emails your receipt, we email what is included, and the team follows up with next steps.

What if I sign up later in the season?

Same price, shorter run. Sign before Oct 24 and a Goodie Bag or Activation package covers every event this quarter.

Can I serve or sell food at my table?

Community Night food is sampling only, unless your business holds the right temporary food event permit.

Is a partnership a donation?

No. It is a marketing partnership: you get a table, goodie bag placement, an activation or a custom program in return.
