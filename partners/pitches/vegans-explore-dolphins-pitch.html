The Plant Based
Showcase

A proposal to bring Vegans Explore's flagship activation into the Dolphins Garage at Hard Rock Stadium this November: local chefs and sponsors showcasing what plant-based living looks like, inside a live home game.

What This Is

A Real Showcase,
Not A Pitch.

We put this event together to showcase what's possible with plant-based cuisine, and to hand out real samples to fans in the stadium who have never tried plant-based food before. No separate ticket required, free-flowing foot traffic on a normal game-day, no on-site sales. That's the whole goal: show how great vegan food can genuinely be, inside a room that would never otherwise walk into a vegan event.
