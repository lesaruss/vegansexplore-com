The Proposal

Three Pillars.
One Game Day
They Remember.

The 2026 Vegan Tailgate Takeover is built on three connected pieces. Each one is designed to deliver value for the Miami Dolphins and for the plant-based, Vegan, and food-allergy community that has been waiting for this moment.

Pillar 01

The F1 Garage Tailgate

The tailgate garage is the only space at Hard Rock Stadium where pedestrians walk by, smell the food, and decide to come in. That visibility is the point. We activate the F1 Garage as a branded, inclusive Vegan tailgate open to all Dolphins fans. Sixty-plus guests, full catering, and a real destination before kickoff.

Pillar 02

The Plant-Based Taste Test Night

We work directly with your F&B vendor. Our team reviews existing sourced inventory and builds a plant-based menu from what they already have. Zero new purchasing required. On game night, those items are sampled by every fan in the stadium. We capture market data throughout and deliver a full research report to the Miami Dolphins after the game.

Pillar 03

The Three-Month Campaign

From the moment the partnership locks, we run a three-month promotion campaign across every channel: social media, email list, in-person events, the Vegans Explore mobile app, paid advertising, and influencer campaigns. The promise is simple: show up to this Dolphins game because we are going to make sure you are fed.

  • Full market research report delivered to the Miami Dolphins within 48 hours post-event

On the Ticket Block

Pre-sale pricing at the 300-level is $95 per seat and expires Friday, May 28. We have a $250 deposit on file and are ready to cover the balance on the initial 10 seats immediately. Every additional seat we add from sponsor and donor commitments is a new ticket sale for the Miami Dolphins at no cost to them.

On the Garage Cost

We are not asking the Miami Dolphins to absorb the venue cost. Our co-sponsor model brings in plant-based brands and wellness companies to offset activation costs. We are also open to structuring sponsor revenue under the Dolphins' existing partnership umbrella so it avoids a separate approval track.

How We Fund It

the Cost.

We run a two-tier co-sponsorship model. The higher tier integrates Miami Dolphins co-branding and deliverables, increasing the value for sponsors. The lower tier focuses on Vegans Explore deliverables. Both tiers offset venue and activation costs with no financial exposure for the Dolphins.

Anchor
Presenting Sponsor

One exclusive presenting sponsor. Full co-branding across all promotion and on-site signage, including Miami Dolphins partnership integration. First right on all future Vegans Explore x Dolphins activations.

Partner
Category Sponsors

Two to three category-specific sponsors covering food, wellness, and lifestyle. Sampling or booth access, social promotion, and event co-branding. Can integrate Dolphins partnership assets at this tier.

Community

501(c)(3) in process. We partner with aligned nonprofits in our network who help promote the event and sell tickets. Community donors contribute to offset the ticket block and event costs.
