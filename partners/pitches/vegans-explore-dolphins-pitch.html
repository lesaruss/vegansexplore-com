Vendor stations, F&B sampling, fresh fruit tastings, plant-based bites, and community programming, open to all Dolphins fans. 200-500 attendees. Two hours pre-game. A real destination that makes fans arrive early and stay engaged.

Three months of promotion before a single fan walks in. Social, email, influencer ambassadors, community events, press releases to 1,000+ contacts, and paid amplification across 8 city markets. Opening day against the Chiefs is the story. We make sure everyone hears it.
