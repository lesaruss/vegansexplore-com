Where This Stands

The Plan, As Confirmed.

Venue: The Garage (locked)

No ticket required, free-flowing foot traffic. Vendors sample and give away, no on-site sales. Our guaranteed home base, we are not waiting on the Overlook.

The Overlook (stretch goal)

Revisited only if we show 600+ pre-committed signups three weeks before the confirmed date. Availability depends on the Dolphins' game-day calendar.

Vendors

  • 10 booths targeted, $200 in tickets each (2-3 tickets per booth)
  • Curated mix: food sampling (Sodexo required for any sale), wellness, general plant-based lifestyle, not food-only
  • Every vendor submitted to Dolphins partnerships for category approval before confirming

Tickets and Revenue

Group Tickets and the Nonprofit Share.

Fevo Group Link

Custom branded link, gated to logged-in Vegans Explore members only. Built-in markup funds the revenue share, for example a $100 ticket at $70 base plus $30 back to Vegans Explore.

Sean's Section

Locked separately using an existing deposit, so a team and content plan can build around a confirmed home base ahead of the group link going live.

Vegans Explore is a 501(c)(3) in process. If it is not finalized by game day, the revenue share routes through one of our existing partner nonprofits instead.

Open and ours to close: confirming the November date, then sending Gabriela a price point and section decision, that unblocks the link build and the seating map.

The Ask

What We Need, What Happens Next.

Date Options, November Window (recommended)

Nov 29, vs. Jets (lead option)

AFC East rivalry, Black Friday weekend draw. Roughly 15 weeks of prep, the most runway of the three for lining up vendors, sponsors, and approvals properly.

Nov 8, vs. Lions

Roughly 12 weeks of prep. Cross-conference matchup with a nationally popular opponent, no holiday-weekend competition for attention.

A third option, November 1 vs. the Patriots (roughly 11 weeks out), is available if a shorter runway works better for the Dolphins.
