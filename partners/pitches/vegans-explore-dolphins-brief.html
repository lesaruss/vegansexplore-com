Purpose: This brief outlines the proposed partnership between Vegans Explore and the Miami Dolphins for a plant-based Opening Day activation on September 27, 2026. It covers the activation model, what each side brings, the vendor ticket structure, track record, and the path toward a long-term year-round collaboration.

The Goal

One game. One year of data. Then more.
We are not coming in for a single night. The goal is to build a relationship: have a strong first outing, demonstrate what this new era of Vegans Explore can deliver, and then regroup. Gather the data. Sit down together. Figure out what a year-round collaboration looks like. This activation is how that conversation starts.
Why Opening Day against the Chiefs
The first home game of the season, against Kansas City, is the highest-profile moment available. It maximizes earned media potential, audience size, and vendor and sponsor interest. A successful activation here becomes the proof of concept for everything that follows.
Platform launches July 13. This partnership launches with it.
Vegans Explore goes live globally on July 13, 2026: South Florida, Tampa, Orlando, Atlanta, New York, Philadelphia, DMV, and London. A confirmed Dolphins partnership is announced at launch, amplifying reach from day one.

Vendor Ticket Model

Each vendor receives a ticket allotment as part of their participation fee. This drives group sales, fills the room with their audience, and adds immediate value to the deal. Sponsorship collaboration is built on top, opening the door for co-branded activations and shared promotional reach. An agreed-upon revenue structure ensures both sides benefit.

Vendor Receives
  • Ticket allotment included in participation fee
  • VE-designed bag inserts and branded materials
  • Audience exposure via VE + Dolphins co-promotion
  • Passport station presence for engagement
Sponsorship Layer
  • Co-branded activation with both VE and Dolphins
  • Social promotion across all VE channels
  • Revenue split on agreed percentage
  • Access to post-event market research report

Track Record

Miami HEAT / Kaseya Center, 2021

South Florida's first Vegan sports arena event. We built a full campaign, sold out our ticket allotment, packed the arena with plant-based fans, and got skybox access. That night led to Full Bloom becoming a permanent Kaseya Center vendor.

Miami Dolphins / Hard Rock Stadium, 2021

Our first Dolphins game day. Ran a focused pilot, sold our ticket allotment, and learned exactly how Hard Rock Stadium operates. We put down a $250 deposit that is still on file. We never walked away.
