The Role

You Are the
Brand in Your City.

A Community Manager is the human face of VEGANS EXPLORE in a specific market. You recruit Vegan businesses as clients, manage their presence on the platform, and build the local community around them. The platform handles the tech. You handle the relationships.

  • Build Your Client Roster Identify and recruit Vegan businesses in your city - restaurants, stores, wellness brands, event spaces. These are your clients. You represent VEGANS EXPLORE to them.
  • Run Brand Audits Every new client starts with a brand audit. You assess their current marketing, online presence, and visibility gaps. The audit is your entry point and your 50% deposit trigger.
  • Manage Their Platform Presence You set up their directory listing, connect them to community events, manage their marketing console, and track their performance metrics. You are their dedicated platform manager.
  • Grow the Local Community You host events, activate the points system locally, and grow the VEGANS EXPLORE membership in your city. A larger community means more value for every client you manage.
  • Report Outcomes, Not Hours You do not bill for time. You deliver results. Directory traffic, event attendance, social reach, membership growth. Your clients pay for what moves the needle.
