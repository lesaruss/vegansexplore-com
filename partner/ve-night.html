The Room

Who's in the Room

Every person in that room is a Passport holder - a platform member by definition. The invite list is curated through our local city contacts, targeting the people who shape community in each city.

Community Influencers

Invites go directly to people with influence in the local vegan community - content creators, organizers, advocates, and business owners. They bring their networks.

Passport Holders

Every ticket purchased is a Passport. Every person in the room is already a platform member. Your audience doesn't end at the door.

City Organizers

Each city is run by a local organizer embedded in that community. They know who matters, who shows up, and how to make the room count.

Online Audience

Virtual tickets extend the event globally. Your brand presence on the event program reaches every screen watching the launch in real time.
