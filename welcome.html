Common questions

Is it a subscription?

No. Founding Membership is one contribution, one time, and it never renews. You can contribute again whenever you like, and every dollar adds 100 points.

Why is there no free membership?

You can look around the Directory, the Pulse, the communities and the Board without an account. Joining is how you take part: posting, replying, voting and reaching people directly. Your contribution is how we grow and keep this running.

Is my contribution a donation?

No. It is a membership contribution to Vegans Explore, not a tax-deductible donation.

What do points do?

Points unlock guides (a guide is 1,100 points) and more over time. You also earn points by taking part.

How does checkout work?

Checkout is secure through Stripe, and Stripe emails your receipt. Your membership and points are added as soon as the payment goes through.
