Eyes and ears

Be the first to know.

When something happens in South Florida’s Vegan community, I want to hear it from you first.

  • A place opening or closing, or a Vegan spot starting to add animal products
  • Whatever people are talking about, and arguing about
  • Tell our assistant on your dashboard. It drafts the story for our Pulse, and you give it a final look. No code, just conversation

Then bring it to the weekly huddle. When a city grows, we all celebrate. When a city stalls, we all rally. If it’s still not working, we talk honestly, and sometimes the role changes hands.
