How partners come in

Say the line, then share the link.

When a business is curious, send them to vegansexplore.com/partners. The page walks them through the rest.

  1. They tap Become a member and make a one-time $11 contribution.
  2. They answer a few quick questions and see their options, with pricing.
  3. They lock in a table or goodie bags, or book a call with me for bigger partnerships.
  4. You get an email with what they chose, and you make sure they get it.

Every partner gets a report within 7 days of each event. That is how we keep our word.
