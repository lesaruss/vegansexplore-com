Bring your show into the network

We're opening the Vegans Explore Podcast Network to other vegan and plant-based shows. The pitch is simple: we promote you, you get exposure to our audience and campaigns, and you stay only as long as it's working for you.

01

We promote your show

Your podcast gets featured across our ad network and outreach campaigns -- the same channels we use to grow our own audience.

02

You share your numbers

Give us visibility into your downloads and audience so we can track real impact and match you with the right campaigns.

03

You join active campaigns

As sponsorship and partner campaigns come through Vegans Explore, network shows get first access to participate.

04

Stay as long as it works

We run it as a trial. If you're not seeing a benefit, you're free to step back -- no contracts, no hard feelings.

🛠 No-risk trial: worst case, you're giving us access to your numbers and staying open to campaigns for a period of time. If it's not delivering value, we part ways -- no harm, no foul.
