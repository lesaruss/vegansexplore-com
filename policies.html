Every policy

Privacy

What we collect, why, who helps us, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address, password (stored scrambled, never readable by us), your city or community, and the Guide you choose.
  • What you tell us: your answers to the Mission Survey, posts and replies on the Community Board, recipes and photos you share, votes, saves, reviews of a place you claim, and messages you send us or ask a Guide.
  • Purchases: what you bought and when. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Check-ins: only when you tap Check in on a listing, your phone shares its location once so we can confirm you are at that place. We keep that location, how precise it was and the time with the check-in.
  • How the site is used: pages viewed, links clicked, and actions on a listing (like calling or getting directions), counted with a random visitor code kept in your browser. We use Google Analytics for site-wide traffic.
  • Email activity: whether an email we sent was delivered, opened or clicked, and whether you unsubscribed.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, membership, Guides, points and levels.
  • To show the Directory, the Board and your city's community, and to keep them honest (for example, a place needs 10 different members' votes to be vetted).
  • To send the emails you asked for, receipts and important account notices.
  • To show businesses how their page is doing (counts, not who you are, unless you check in, below).
  • To keep the site safe, stop spam and abuse, and fix problems.

Who can see what

  • Your public posts, recipes and approved photos show with your first name and last initial.
  • Check-ins: the public sees counts only. Other members see the names of members who leave "Show my check-ins to other members" on (My Account, Privacy; it is on by default). The owner of a claimed listing can see who checked in at their place, whatever that setting says, so they can thank and reward regulars.
  • Your city's Community Manager and our team can see what they need to help you and to keep the community safe.

Who helps us run the site

We use trusted services to host the site and database, take payments (Stripe), send email, measure traffic (Google Analytics), look up public business details (such as Google's business listings and businesses' public social profiles), and power our AI features (see How we use AI). They get only what they need to do that job for us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, except what we must keep for legal, tax or safety reasons.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom of it.
  • Turn off "Show my check-ins to other members" in My Account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account: email contact@lesaruss.com from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Vegans Explore is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 made an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, scrambled passwords and access limits for our team. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

How we use AI

We use AI to keep a large community running with a small team. Here is where, what it means for you, and the lines we don't cross.

Our Guides are AI characters

Liz, Maya, River, Nori, Theo, Dani and the other Guides are characters we created. They are not real people. Their pictures are illustrations, and their voices and video clips are made with AI tools.

When you ask a Guide a question, we first look for an answer our team has already written and checked. If there isn't one and you choose to ask the Guide directly, an AI writes the reply. Those replies are saved as drafts, and a draft becomes a saved answer only after it is checked. You can always choose Ask a person instead.

AI can be wrong

AI replies can contain mistakes. Nothing a Guide says is medical, nutrition, legal or financial advice. Talk to a qualified professional about your health, diet, or any big decision. If a Guide says something wrong, tell us and we will fix the saved answer.

Where else we use AI

  • The Daily Pulse: each day's topics are drafted with AI from real, linked sources and can be reviewed or pulled by our team.
  • The Directory: AI helps write short descriptions and copy menus and hours from a business's own website and public profiles. We never invent details, and an owner can edit their page at any time.
  • Pictures: some pictures are made with AI. Pictures of food or products made this way are labeled "Illustrative photo" until real ones replace them.
  • Business audits and check-ups: automated checks read a business's public website, Google listing and social profiles to suggest fixes and to flag places that may have closed. A person reviews a flag before a listing is marked closed.
  • Podcast transcripts: our episodes are transcribed and tagged by topic with AI so you can search them.

The lines we don't cross

  • We don't sell your information, and we don't train AI models on it.
  • We don't present an AI character as a real person, and we don't make AI pictures of real people without their permission.
  • A person makes the calls that matter: approving claims, vetting listings, removing posts and paying out prizes.

Membership, points and payments

How the Passport, Guides, points and levels work.

The Passport

  • The Passport is $11, one time. It makes you a member and comes with 1,100 welcome points. There is no monthly fee.
  • Any purchase of $11 or more (a Guide, an event ticket, merchandise) also makes you a member.
  • Founding Membership "member since" dates never move while your membership is active.

Guides

Each Guide is $11 or 1,100 points, and it's yours to keep. Free Guides stay free for everyone.

Listings and claims

Submitting a business to the Directory, or claiming a business's page, is $11 or 1,100 points each. The Passport's 1,100 welcome points cover your first one, or anything else points do: you choose. We review every one by hand.

Points

  • Points are given for taking part: Bounties, campaigns, the Daily Pulse, the Community Board, check-ins and referrals. Every purchase also earns a 25-point thank-you.
  • Points can't be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out, and they have no cash value. You can spend them on Guides, Guide chat, and submitting or claiming listings, or give them to a business or nonprofit on the Directory as a boost, never as money.
  • Levels go by lifetime points: Level 2 from 2,500, Level 3 from 10,000, Level 4 from 25,000. The team can start someone at a higher level. Spending or giving points never lowers your level.
  • We may change how points are earned or spent, and we can take back points earned by breaking the terms.

Contributions

Contributions to Vegans Explore support the community: events, Community Managers and the apps. They go to LESARUSS Inc., a company, so they are not tax-deductible donations.

Payments and questions about a charge

Payments are processed by Stripe. If a charge looks wrong or something you bought didn't work, email contact@lesaruss.com with your receipt and we will sort it out.

Community guidelines

The Community Board, the Daily Pulse, recipes and photos work because people are kind and honest. These rules keep it that way.

Do

  • Be welcoming, especially to people who are just starting out. Everyone's journey is different.
  • Share what you know, with a source when it's a fact.
  • Keep posts in the right section: Requests and Offers, Jobs, Classifieds, Fosters, Rescues, or Suggest a Topic.

Don't

  • Harass, threaten, shame or bully anyone, or attack people for who they are.
  • Post spam, scams, false information, or anything illegal.
  • Share someone else's private information, or photos you don't have the right to share.
  • Sell or rehome animals for profit. The Fosters and Rescues boards are for placing animals in care, never selling them.
  • Post graphic content without a warning, or anything sexual.

Reporting

If you see something that breaks these rules, email contact@lesaruss.com with a link to it. Our team and your city's Community Manager review reports and can remove posts or close accounts.

Businesses and listings

How places get on the Directory, and what owners can do.

  • How listings are made: from public information (the business's own website, Google's business listing and public social profiles), from members' suggestions, and from owners who apply. We never invent details.
  • Vetting: a Vegan-friendly or Vegan-options food place shows publicly once 10 different members vote for it, its owner claims it, or our team vets it.
  • Claiming: owners claim their page with the $11 Founding Membership or the Partner plan. Once our team approves the claim, the owner can edit the page, menu, hours and photos.
  • Votes and points can't be bought. Rankings come from members.
  • Closed or wrong? Tell us and we will check. Places that close for good are kept under Closed, for memory.
  • Emails to businesses: we may email a business at the address it publishes on its own website, about its page. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and we stop for good when asked.
  • Remove a listing: owners can ask us to take a page down by emailing contact@lesaruss.com.

Cookies and analytics

What we store in your browser and why.

  • Keeping you signed in: your sign-in is kept in your browser's storage so you don't have to sign in on every page.
  • Remembering your choices: things like your city, a folded banner, a shopping list or a quiz in progress.
  • Counting visits: a random visitor code so a listing's numbers count people, not page refreshes.
  • Google Analytics: Google's cookies measure site-wide traffic for us. You can block them with your browser settings or Google's opt-out add-on.
  • Tracked links: links we send look like vegansexplore.com/go/... so we know which ones work. Link previews and scanners are not counted.

We don't put ad-network trackers (like Facebook or TikTok pixels) on the site. Clearing your browser's site data signs you out and resets these choices.

Accessibility

Vegans Explore should work for everyone.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2 at level AA: readable contrast, pages that work with a keyboard and a screen reader, captions or words alongside our Guide clips, and layouts that work on a phone. We check new pages against those guidelines, and we fix what we find.

If something on the site doesn't work for you, email contact@lesaruss.com with the page and what happened. We will fix it, or get you what you need another way.
